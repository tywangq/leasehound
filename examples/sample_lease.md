# Residential Lease Agreement

This Residential Lease Agreement is entered into between Evergreen Pines Properties LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned Tenant for the premises located at 1234 Pine Street, Apartment 5B, Seattle, WA 98101.

1. TERM. The initial term of this lease is twelve (12) months, commencing September 1, converting to month-to-month tenancy thereafter unless renewed in writing by both parties.

2. RENT. Monthly rent is $1,850, due on or before the first day of each calendar month. Rent covers occupancy of the premises and building common areas.

3. LATE CHARGES. If rent is not received by 11:59 PM on the first day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $75, plus an additional $10 for each day rent remains unpaid thereafter.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,850, held in a trust account at Sound Community Bank. The deposit is refundable subject to the terms of the move-in condition checklist signed by both parties.

5. ATTORNEYS' FEES. In any action or dispute arising out of this Agreement, Tenant agrees to pay all of Landlord's attorneys' fees and costs incurred, regardless of the outcome of such action.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for electricity, internet, and any personal subscriptions. Landlord pays for water, sewer, and garbage collection service.

7. ENTRY. Landlord or Landlord's agents may enter the premises at any time and without prior notice for the purposes of inspection, maintenance, repairs, or showing the unit to prospective tenants or buyers.

8. MAINTENANCE. Tenant shall keep the premises clean and sanitary, dispose of garbage properly, and promptly notify Landlord of any needed repairs or unsafe conditions.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY. Tenant agrees not to disclose to any person the rent amount, concessions, move-in incentives, or any other terms of this lease agreement.

10. TERMINATION BY TENANT. After the initial term, Tenant may terminate the month-to-month tenancy by providing at least twenty (20) days' written notice prior to the end of a rental period.

11. WAIVER OF RIGHTS. Tenant hereby waives all rights and remedies available under chapter 59.18 RCW to the fullest extent permitted, and agrees not to join or participate in any class action against Landlord.

12. SMOKE DETECTION DEVICES. The premises are equipped with smoke detection devices. Tenant is responsible for maintaining the devices in working order, including battery replacement, as specified by the manufacturer.

13. PAYMENT METHOD. Rent shall be paid exclusively through electronic transfer via the Landlord's designated online payment portal. No cash, check, or money order payments will be accepted under any circumstances.

14. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Landlord shall not be liable for any injury, loss, or damage to persons or property occurring on the premises from any cause whatsoever, and Tenant agrees to indemnify and hold Landlord harmless against all such claims, including Landlord's legal costs.
