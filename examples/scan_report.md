## 🚩 Red

### Clause 4: 3. LATE CHARGES. If rent is not received by 11:59 PM on the first day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $7…

Washington law says landlords cannot charge late fees if rent is paid within five days after the due date. This lease charges a $75 late fee starting the day after rent is due, without allowing the five-day grace period, which conflicts with the law.
- RCW 59.18.170 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.170

### Clause 6: 5. ATTORNEYS' FEES. In any action or dispute arising out of this Agreement, Tenant agrees to pay all of Landlord's attor…

The clause requires the tenant to pay all of the landlord's attorney fees regardless of outcome, but Washington law generally allows only the prevailing party to recover reasonable attorney fees in disputes. This clause conflicts with statutes that limit attorney fee awards to the winning party and include exceptions, so it is likely unenforceable.
- RCW 59.18.250 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.250
- RCW 59.18.510 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.510
- RCW 59.18.290 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.290
- RCW 59.18.270 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.270

### Clause 8: 7. ENTRY. Landlord or Landlord's agents may enter the premises at any time and without prior notice for the purposes of …

The lease clause allows the landlord to enter the rental unit at any time and without prior notice, but Washington law requires landlords to provide at least two days' written notice before entry except in emergencies. Therefore, the clause conflicts with the law because it does not require notice before entry.
- RCW 59.18.150(6) — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.150

### Clause 10: 9. CONFIDENTIALITY. Tenant agrees not to disclose to any person the rent amount, concessions, move-in incentives, or any…

The lease clause requires the tenant to keep rent and lease terms confidential, which is not allowed under Washington law. Specifically, RCW 59.18.230(2)(c) prohibits rental agreements from including nondisclosure agreements about rent amounts, concessions, or lease terms. Therefore, this confidentiality clause conflicts with the law and is unenforceable.
- RCW 59.18.230(2)(c) — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.230

### Clause 12: 11. WAIVER OF RIGHTS. Tenant hereby waives all rights and remedies available under chapter 59.18 RCW to the fullest exte…

The clause tries to make the tenant give up rights under the landlord-tenant law and stop them from joining class actions, which is not allowed. Washington law says such waivers are against public policy and cannot be enforced.
- RCW 59.18.230 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.230

### Clause 14: 13. PAYMENT METHOD. Rent shall be paid exclusively through electronic transfer via the Landlord's designated online paym…

Washington law requires landlords to accept personal checks, cashier's checks, or money orders for rent payments unless the tenant has recently bounced checks. This lease clause prohibits all payment methods except electronic transfer, which conflicts with the law's requirement to accept checks or money orders.
- RCW 59.18.063 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.063

### Clause 15: 14. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Landlord shall not be liable for any injury, loss, or damage to persons or property occurri…

The clause tries to completely absolve the landlord from liability for any injury or damage on the premises, but Washington law requires landlords to maintain the property in a safe and habitable condition. Landlords cannot waive their responsibility to keep the premises safe, so this clause conflicts with the landlord's legal duties under RCW 59.18.060.
- RCW 59.18.060 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.060

## ⚠️ Yellow

### Clause 5: 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,850, held in a trust account at Sound Community Bank. The…

The clause states the security deposit is held in a trust account, which aligns with the law requiring deposits to be held in a trust account and the tenant to be notified. However, the clause does not mention providing a written move-in condition checklist to the tenant, which is required before collecting a deposit. Also, the lease should specify conditions under which the deposit may be withheld, which is not addressed here. Therefore, the clause is potentially problematic and may not fully comply with the law.
- RCW 59.18.260 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.260
- RCW 59.18.270 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.270

## 🔍 Missing protections

Required by law but not found in this lease — worth asking your landlord:

- **Mold information** — The landlord must provide written information about the health hazards of indoor mold and how to control mold growth. (RCW 59.18.060)
