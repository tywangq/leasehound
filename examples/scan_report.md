## 🚩 Red

### Clause 4: 3. LATE CHARGES. If rent is not received by 11:59 PM on the first day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of …

Washington law says landlords cannot charge late fees if rent is paid within five days after the due date. This lease charges a $75 late fee starting the day after rent is due, without allowing the five-day grace period, which conflicts with the law.
- RCW 59.18.170 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.170

### Clause 6: 5. ATTORNEYS' FEES. In any action or dispute arising out of this Agreement, Tenant agrees to pay all of Landlord's …

The clause requires the tenant to pay all of the landlord's attorney fees regardless of the outcome, but Washington law generally allows only the prevailing party to recover reasonable attorney fees in disputes. This clause conflicts with the law because it imposes fees on the tenant even if the tenant wins or the landlord does not prevail.
- RCW 59.18.250 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.250
- RCW 59.18.510 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.510
- RCW 59.18.290 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.290
- RCW 59.18.270 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.270

### Clause 8: 7. ENTRY. Landlord or Landlord's agents may enter the premises at any time and without prior notice for the purposes of …

The lease clause allows the landlord to enter the rental unit at any time and without prior notice, but Washington law requires landlords to provide at least two days' written notice before entry except in emergencies. Therefore, the clause conflicts with the law because it does not require notice before entry.
- RCW 59.18.150(6) — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.150

### Clause 10: 9. CONFIDENTIALITY. Tenant agrees not to disclose to any person the rent amount, concessions, move-in incentives, or …

The lease clause requires the tenant to keep rent and lease terms confidential, which is prohibited by Washington law. Specifically, RCW 59.18.230(2)(c) forbids rental agreements from including nondisclosure agreements about rent amounts, concessions, or lease terms. Therefore, this confidentiality clause is not allowed and unenforceable.
- RCW 59.18.230(2)(c) — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.230

### Clause 12: 11. WAIVER OF RIGHTS. Tenant hereby waives all rights and remedies available under chapter 59.18 RCW to the fullest …

The clause tries to make the tenant give up rights and not join class actions, which is not allowed under Washington law. Such waivers are against public policy and cannot be enforced.
- RCW 59.18.230 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.230

### Clause 14: 13. PAYMENT METHOD. Rent shall be paid exclusively through electronic transfer via the Landlord's designated online …

Washington law requires landlords to accept personal checks, cashier's checks, or money orders for rent payments unless the tenant has recently bounced checks. This lease clause prohibits all payment methods except electronic transfer, which conflicts with the law's requirement to accept these payment forms.
- RCW 59.18.063 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.063

### Clause 15: 14. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Landlord shall not be liable for any injury, loss, or damage to persons or property …

The lease clause tries to completely exempt the landlord from liability for any injury or damage on the premises, but Washington law requires landlords to maintain the property in a safe and habitable condition. Landlords cannot contract away their legal duty to keep the premises safe, so this clause conflicts with the landlord's responsibilities under RCW 59.18.060.
- RCW 59.18.060 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.060

## ⚠️ Yellow

### Clause 5: 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,850, held in a trust account at Sound Community Bank. …

The clause states the security deposit is held in a trust account, which aligns with the law requiring deposits to be held in a trust account and the tenant to be notified. However, the clause does not mention providing a written receipt or a detailed move-in condition checklist, which the law requires before collecting a deposit. Without confirmation that these were provided, the clause may be problematic.
- RCW 59.18.260 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.260
- RCW 59.18.270 — https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.270

## 🔍 Missing protections

Required by law but not found in this lease — worth asking your landlord:

- **Fire safety information** — The landlord must provide written fire safety and protection information (smoke detection, escape routes) at the start of the tenancy. (RCW 59.18.060)
- **Mold information** — The landlord must provide written information about the health hazards of indoor mold and how to control mold growth. (RCW 59.18.060)
