# Apartment Rental Contract

This Rental Contract is between Northgate Residential Partners ("Landlord") and Tenant for Apartment 3C at 415 Birch Boulevard, Spokane, WA 99201.

1. TERM. The tenancy begins June 1 for an initial period of one year, converting to a month-to-month arrangement at the end of the initial period.

2. RENT. Rent is $1,275 per month, payable in advance on or before the first calendar day of each month by check, money order, or the online portal.

3. LATE CHARGES. Rent not received by the end of the second day of the month is subject to a late fee of $60, with an additional $5 for each further day the balance remains outstanding.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall deposit $1,275 as security. The deposit may be withheld only for unpaid rent, damages beyond normal wear and tear, or costs of restoring the unit as documented after move-out.

5. MOVE-IN INSPECTION. Landlord and Tenant have jointly completed and signed a written statement describing the condition and cleanliness of the unit and its furnishings, and Tenant holds a copy of that statement.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant pays electricity, gas, and internet. Landlord pays water, sewer, and trash collection for the building.

7. ACCESS. Landlord may enter the apartment for inspections, maintenance, or showings upon giving Tenant twelve (12) hours' advance notice, at any hour Landlord deems reasonable.

8. MAINTENANCE. Tenant agrees to keep the apartment clean, operate appliances reasonably, and report leaks, pests, or hazards to management without delay.

9. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receiving the Department of Health pamphlet on indoor mold, its health effects, and how to prevent and control mold growth in the unit.

10. TERMINATION. After the initial term, Tenant may terminate with twenty (20) days' written notice before the end of any rental period, and Landlord may terminate only as permitted by law.

11. RULES. Tenant shall observe the building rules posted in the lobby, which Landlord may amend from time to time consistent with applicable law and with reasonable notice to tenants.
