# Lease Agreement — Maple Court Apartments

This Lease Agreement is entered into by Maple Court Apartments LLC ("Landlord") and Tenant for Unit 7A, 2200 Maple Court, Bellevue, WA 98004.

1. TERM. The lease term is nine (9) months commencing October 1, after which the tenancy continues month-to-month unless renewed or terminated as the law allows.

2. RENT. Monthly rent is $2,100, due the first of each month. A five-day grace period applies; a $50 late charge may be assessed starting the sixth day rent remains unpaid.

3. CONFIDENTIAL TERMS. Tenant shall not discuss or reveal the rental rate, discounts, or lease concessions to other residents of the building or to any third party, whether during or after the tenancy.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a $2,100 security deposit. The deposit may be withheld only for unpaid rent, damage beyond ordinary wear and tear, or other sums owed under this lease, itemized in writing after move-out.

5. DEPOSIT ACCOUNT. The deposit is held in a trust account at First Cascade Credit Union, 500 Main Street, Bellevue, WA, and Tenant will be notified in writing if the depository changes.

6. CONDITION REPORT. The parties have completed and signed a written move-in condition checklist covering walls, flooring, appliances, and fixtures; Tenant acknowledges receiving a copy.

7. DISPUTES. Any claim arising from this lease shall be brought by Tenant individually; Tenant waives the right to join, initiate, or participate in any class or representative action against Landlord.

8. PAYMENT METHOD. All rent and other charges shall be paid solely through the Zelle transfer service to Landlord's designated account. No other form of payment, including checks or money orders, will be accepted.

9. ENTRY. Except in emergencies, Landlord will provide at least two (2) days' written notice before entry for repairs or inspection, and one (1) day's notice to show the unit, entering only at reasonable times.

10. FIRE AND SAFETY. Tenant has received written fire safety information for the building, including smoke detector locations and maintenance duties, evacuation routes, and the fire protection plan.

11. MOLD. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information about the health hazards of indoor mold and recommended steps to prevent and control mold growth in the dwelling.
