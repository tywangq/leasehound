# Month-to-Month Rental Agreement

This Rental Agreement is between J. Alvarez ("Landlord") and Tenant for the basement apartment at 17 Juniper Lane, Olympia, WA 98501.

1. TERM. This is a month-to-month tenancy beginning on the first day of next month and continuing until terminated by either party as provided by law.

2. RENT. Rent is $995 per month, due on the first. Tenant may pay by check, money order, or bank transfer. No security deposit, pet deposit, or move-in fee is collected under this Agreement.

3. LATE CHARGES. If rent is unpaid more than five days after the due date, Tenant owes a single late charge of $25. Landlord will provide any required notice before pursuing further remedies.

4. REMEDIES. Tenant agrees that the remedies and enforcement procedures provided to tenants by chapter 59.18 RCW shall not apply to this tenancy, and that the parties' obligations are defined solely by this Agreement.

5. ENTRY. Except in emergencies, Landlord will give at least two (2) days' written notice before entering, and entries will occur at reasonable times agreed with Tenant where practicable.

6. MAINTENANCE. Tenant shall keep the apartment clean, avoid damaging the premises, and promptly tell Landlord about leaks, electrical problems, or other conditions needing repair.

7. FIRE SAFETY. Tenant has received written fire safety information for the dwelling, including the location and maintenance of the smoke detection device and the recommended emergency exit route.

8. UTILITIES. Rent includes water, sewer, and garbage service. Tenant pays for electricity and internet service separately in Tenant's own name.

9. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this month-to-month tenancy as allowed by Washington law; Tenant shall give at least twenty (20) days' written notice before the end of a rental period.
