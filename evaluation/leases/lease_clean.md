# Residential Lease Agreement

This Residential Lease Agreement is made between Cascade View Homes LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned Tenant for the dwelling at 88 Alder Way, Unit 12, Tacoma, WA 98402.

1. TERM. This lease runs for twelve (12) months beginning March 1, and continues month-to-month afterward unless either party ends it as provided by law or the parties sign a renewal.

2. RENT. Monthly rent is $1,600, due on the first day of each month. Rent may be paid by personal check, cashier's check, money order, or electronic transfer, at Tenant's choice.

3. LATE CHARGES. If rent remains unpaid more than five (5) days after the due date, Tenant shall pay a one-time late charge of $40. No late charge accrues during the first five days after rent is due.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,600. The deposit may be withheld only for unpaid rent, damage beyond ordinary wear and tear, or other tenant obligations under this lease, as itemized in a written statement after move-out.

5. DEPOSIT LOCATION. The security deposit will be held in a trust account at Harborview Savings Bank, 200 Commerce Street, Tacoma, WA. Tenant will be notified in writing of any change to the depository.

6. MOVE-IN CHECKLIST. A written checklist describing the condition of the unit, including fixtures, appliances, carpets, and paint, has been completed and signed by both Landlord and Tenant, and a copy provided to Tenant.

7. ENTRY. Except in emergencies, Landlord shall give Tenant at least two (2) days' written notice before entering to inspect or make repairs, and at least one (1) day's notice to show the unit to prospective tenants or purchasers. Entry will occur at reasonable times.

8. MAINTENANCE. Tenant shall keep the unit clean and sanitary, dispose of garbage properly, use fixtures reasonably, and promptly report needed repairs or unsafe conditions to Landlord.

9. FIRE SAFETY. Landlord has provided Tenant with written fire safety and protection information, including the location of smoke detection devices, emergency escape routes, and the building's fire evacuation plan.

10. MOLD DISCLOSURE. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Washington State Department of Health information about the health hazards of indoor mold and steps tenants and landlords can take to control mold growth.

11. TERMINATION BY TENANT. After the initial term, Tenant may end the month-to-month tenancy with at least twenty (20) days' written notice before the end of a rental period.

12. QUIET ENJOYMENT. Landlord shall not interfere with Tenant's peaceful use and enjoyment of the premises, subject to the entry provisions above and Landlord's legal rights and duties.
