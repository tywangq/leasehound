# Residential Tenancy Agreement

This Agreement is made between Sound Ridge Property Management ("Landlord") and Tenant for the house at 92 Ridgeline Drive, Everett, WA 98201.

1. TERM. The tenancy is for twelve (12) months beginning January 15 and continues thereafter on a month-to-month basis unless terminated in accordance with this Agreement or applicable law.

2. RENT. Rent is $2,450 per month, due on the first. Payments may be made by check, money order, or electronic transfer through the resident portal.

3. LATE CHARGES. If rent is not received by 9:00 AM on the first day of the month, a late charge of $95 applies immediately, plus $15 per day until the full balance including charges is received.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,450, refundable subject to the deductions described in the signed move-in condition checklist and this Agreement's terms on unpaid rent and damages.

5. LEGAL COSTS. In any dispute, action, or proceeding arising from this Agreement or the tenancy, Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for all attorneys' fees, court costs, and collection expenses Landlord incurs, whether or not Landlord prevails.

6. RELEASE. Landlord and its agents shall not be responsible for any injury to persons or loss of or damage to property on the premises, however caused, and Tenant releases Landlord from all such liability and agrees to defend and indemnify Landlord against related claims.

7. STATUTORY WAIVER. To the maximum extent permitted, Tenant waives the rights, remedies, and protections afforded to tenants under chapter 59.18 RCW, and agrees this Agreement alone governs the parties' obligations.

8. ACCESS. Landlord and its contractors may enter the premises at reasonable times without advance notice to Tenant for inspection, maintenance, repairs, or showing the property.

9. MAINTENANCE. Tenant shall maintain the premises in a clean and sanitary condition, replace furnace filters as scheduled, maintain the yard, and promptly notify Landlord of required repairs.

10. FIRE SAFETY INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receiving written fire safety and protection information, including smoke detector locations and testing duties and the recommended escape routes for the dwelling.

11. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receiving the Department of Health written materials regarding indoor mold hazards and the measures occupants can take to prevent mold growth.

12. TERMINATION. After the initial term, either party may end the month-to-month tenancy only as permitted by law; Tenant shall give at least twenty (20) days' written notice before the end of a rental period.
