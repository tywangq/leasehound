This lease agreement is made between John Shepard ("Landlord") and Maria Torres ("Tenant") for the rental of the house located at 1223 E 35th Street, Tacoma, Washington. The Tenant agrees to lease the premises according to the following terms:

1. TERM. The lease term begins July 1, 2024, and lasts for twelve (12) months, ending June 30, 2025. Should the Tenant wish to remain after the end of the term, both parties will need to agree to renew or extend the lease in writing.

2. RENT. Monthly rent is $2,075, due on the first day of each month. Rent not received by the fifth (5th) of the month is considered late and will incur a late fee of $50.

3. RENT PAYMENT METHOD. Rent must be paid exclusively through Zelle to the Landlord’s email account. No checks, money orders, or cash payments will be accepted under any circumstances. All rent must be paid electronically via this method.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant will pay a security deposit of $2,075 upon signing this lease. The deposit is held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 401 Broadway, Tacoma, WA 98402.

5. DEPOSIT WITHHOLDING. The deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, for damage beyond normal wear and tear, or for any other charges owed under this lease. Within 21 days of move-out, the Landlord will provide an itemized statement of any deductions.

6. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Upon move-in, Tenant and Landlord will sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises. The checklist will serve as a record for comparison upon move-out.

7. OCCUPANTS. Only those persons listed and approved in writing by the Landlord may reside in the premises. Guests may not stay longer than 14 days in any 90-day period without written consent from the Landlord.

8. USE OF PREMISES. The premises will be used solely as a residence. No business or commercial activity may be conducted without the Landlord’s written permission.

9. PETS. No pets are allowed without the prior written approval of the Landlord. Any approved pets may require an additional deposit.

10. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the premises clean and in good repair. Tenant must promptly notify the Landlord of any damage or needed repairs. Landlord will be responsible for repairs not caused by tenant negligence or misuse.

11. LANDLORD ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises for inspection, repairs, or to show the property to prospective tenants or buyers, with at least two (2) days’ notice, except in emergencies.

12. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for paying all utilities, including water, sewer, electricity, trash, and internet, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

13. FIRE SAFETY. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information regarding fire safety, evacuation plans, and smoke detector maintenance.

14. CONFIDENTIALITY. Tenant agrees not to disclose or discuss the amount of rent or any terms of this lease with anyone outside the household without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

15. LIABILITY. Landlord will not be liable for any injury to the Tenant or their guests, or for any damage to their property occurring on the premises, even if such injury or damage is caused by the Landlord’s own negligence. The Tenant agrees to hold the Landlord harmless from any and all claims for damages or injury occurring on the premises during the lease term.
