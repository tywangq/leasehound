This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between Robert L. Hanley ("Landlord") and Dana T. Kim ("Tenant"), relating to the premises located at 1810 Meridian Street, Bellingham, Washington 98225 (the "Premises").

1. TERM. The term of this Agreement shall commence on July 1, 2024 and continue on a month-to-month basis unless terminated in accordance with this Agreement and applicable law. Tenant shall not occupy the premises prior to the commencement date.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent in the amount of $3,025.00, due on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month. Rent shall be paid to Landlord at the address specified in this Agreement or to such other place as Landlord may designate in writing.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,025.00 to be held by Landlord. The security deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, damage to the Premises beyond normal wear and tear, or other lawful charges, subject to applicable law. Any deductions shall be itemized and provided to Tenant in writing.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION. At move-in, Landlord and Tenant shall execute a written checklist or statement specifying the condition and cleanliness of the Premises, which shall be signed by both parties and retained in Landlord’s records. This checklist shall be used to determine the condition of the Premises at move-out.

5. OCCUPANTS AND USE. The Premises shall be used solely for residential purposes by Tenant and the following occupants: none in addition to Tenant without prior written consent of Landlord. No commercial activity is permitted on the Premises.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all utilities and services to the Premises, including but not limited to electricity, water, sewer, and garbage unless otherwise stated herein. Failure to pay utilities may constitute a material breach.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and in good order and shall immediately notify Landlord of any required repairs or hazardous conditions. Tenant shall be liable for any damage caused by Tenant’s negligence or misuse.

8. ENTRY AND INSPECTION. Landlord or Landlord’s agents may enter the Premises for the purpose of inspection, repairs, or showing the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers by providing Tenant with twelve (12) hours’ prior notice, except in the case of emergency when no notice is required.

9. PETS. No pets shall be kept on the Premises without Landlord’s prior written consent. Any approved pet shall be subject to additional terms and conditions as specified by Landlord.

10. FIRE SAFETY AND DETECTORS. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety information and instructions regarding smoke detector operation, testing, and maintenance as required by RCW 43.44.110.

11. ALTERATIONS. Tenant shall not make alterations, improvements, or additions to the Premises without Landlord’s prior written approval. All approved alterations shall become the property of Landlord upon termination of tenancy.

12. SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not assign this Agreement or sublet any part of the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord. Any unauthorized assignment or subletting shall constitute a material breach.

13. ATTORNEY FEES. In the event of any dispute or litigation arising out of this Agreement, Tenant agrees to pay Landlord’s reasonable attorney fees and costs, regardless of which party prevails in any action.
