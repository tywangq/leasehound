This lease agreement is made between John Peterson (Landlord) and Maria Hernandez (Tenant) for the rental of the house located at 3423 E Sprague Ave, Spokane, WA 99202. The property will be occupied as a residence only, beginning July 1, 2024.

1. RENT. Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of $2300, due on the first day of each month. Payment shall be made to the Landlord at the address provided, or electronically if mutually agreed.

2. TERM. The lease term will begin on July 1, 2024, and continue on a month-to-month basis unless either party gives at least 20 days' written notice of intent to terminate prior to the end of a rental period.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2300. The deposit is held in a trust account at Spokane Teachers Credit Union, 106 W Nora Ave, Spokane, WA 99205. The deposit may be withheld or deducted only for unpaid rent or to repair damages beyond normal wear and tear.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Upon moving in, Tenant and Landlord will complete, sign, and date a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises. Both parties shall retain a copy of the signed checklist for their records.

5. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for paying for electricity, water, sewer, garbage, internet, and cable services. Landlord will pay for property taxes and homeowner’s insurance only.

6. LATE FEES. Rent not received by the fifth day after it is due is subject to a late fee of $50. No late fee will be charged if rent is paid within the five-day grace period after the due date.

7. PETS AND PET DEPOSIT. Pets are allowed only with prior written consent from the Landlord. If a pet is approved, an additional refundable pet damage deposit of $500 is required. The pet deposit may be withheld or deducted only for damage caused by the pet beyond normal wear and tear.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord of any needed repairs. Landlord will be responsible for maintenance and repair of the premises except for damage caused by Tenant’s negligence.

9. TENANT RESPONSIBILITY FOR NEGLIGENCE. Tenant is responsible for the cost of repairs required due to damage caused by the Tenant’s own negligence or misuse, or that of their guests.

10. QUIET ENJOYMENT. Landlord agrees that Tenant shall have quiet enjoyment of the premises and will not disturb Tenant’s use and enjoyment, so long as all lease terms are met.

11. HOUSE RULES. Tenant agrees to comply with house rules provided in writing by the Landlord, which may be reasonably amended by the Landlord with at least two weeks’ written notice.

12. ENTRY. Landlord will provide Tenant with at least two days’ written notice before entering the premises, except in case of emergency. Entry will be at reasonable times to inspect, repair, or show the property.

13. ALTERATIONS. Tenant may not make alterations or improvements to the property without Landlord’s prior written consent, except for minor decorating that does not damage the premises.

14. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Department of Health information on mold, including guidance on controlling, preventing, and responding to mold growth in residential units.

15. SUBLETTING. The Tenant shall not sublease or assign the premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Any unauthorized subletting may result in termination of this agreement.
