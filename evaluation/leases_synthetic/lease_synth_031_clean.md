This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between Cascade Property Solutions LLC ("Landlord") and Emily Tran ("Tenant") for the residential premises located at 1823 Pinecone Avenue, Bellingham, Washington 98225 (the "Premises").

1. TERM AND OCCUPANCY. The tenancy shall commence on July 1, 2024 and continue on a month-to-month basis unless terminated in accordance with this Agreement or applicable law. Only those persons listed in the rental application may occupy the Premises.

2. RENT. Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of $1,500.00, due on the first day of each month. Rent shall be delivered to Landlord at the address specified for payment or via electronic transfer as directed by Landlord. There is no grace period; rent is due on the first of the month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00 upon signing this Agreement. The deposit may be withheld to cover unpaid rent, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other lawful charges expressly permitted by RCW 59.18. Any remaining balance will be returned in accord with Washington law.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Upon move-in, Tenant and Landlord shall complete and sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the Premises, including all systems and appliances. Tenant acknowledges receipt of a copy of this checklist at the commencement of tenancy.

5. PETS AND PET DAMAGE DEPOSIT. No pets shall reside on the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. If a pet is approved, Tenant agrees to pay an additional refundable pet damage deposit of $350.00. This deposit may be withheld for damages caused by Tenant’s pet(s) beyond normal wear and tear, in accordance with RCW 59.18.285.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall maintain the Premises in a clean and sanitary condition and shall promptly notify Landlord of any conditions requiring repair. Tenant is responsible for any damage to the Premises caused by Tenant’s negligence, intentional act, or that of Tenant’s guests, excepting ordinary wear and tear.

7. ENTRY BY LANDLORD. Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs, or showings with two (2) days’ written notice to Tenant, except in case of emergency. For showings to prospective tenants or purchasers, one day’s advance written notice will be provided, as permitted by law.

8. TERMINATION BY TENANT. Tenant may terminate this tenancy by providing the Landlord with at least twenty (20) days’ written notice prior to the end of a rental period, in accordance with RCW 59.18.200.

9. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTOR INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety and smoke detector information as required by RCW 59.18.060.

10. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Department of Health’s informational pamphlet on the hazards associated with indoor mold as required by RCW 59.18.060.
