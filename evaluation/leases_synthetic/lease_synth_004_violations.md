This lease agreement is made between Rachel Meyers (Landlord) and Jordan Lee (Tenant) for the rental of the single-family residence at 1510 Willow Avenue, Everett, Washington.

1. TERM AND POSSESSION. The rental term will begin on July 1, 2024, and will continue month-to-month unless terminated by either party with 30 days’ written notice. Possession of the premises will be delivered to the Tenant on the start date.

2. RENT. The monthly rent is $2,775, due in full on the first day of each month at the Landlord’s address or by electronic transfer. Rent must be paid on time every month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,000 prior to moving in. The deposit will be held in a trust account at Coastal Community Bank, 10520 19th Ave SE, Everett, WA 98208. The deposit may be withheld to cover unpaid rent or costs for damage to the unit beyond normal wear and tear. Any remaining balance will be returned to the Tenant within 21 days after move-out.

4. LATE FEES. If the full rent is not received by the first day it is due, Tenant agrees to pay a late fee of $75. This fee is assessed on the first day rent is late and is in addition to the rent owed.

5. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for payment of all utilities, including electricity, water, sewer, garbage, gas, and internet. Landlord is not responsible for any utility bills not paid by Tenant.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the premises in clean, safe condition and promptly notify Landlord of any needed repairs. Tenant is responsible for the cost of repairs due to misuse or neglect.

7. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises with at least 2 days’ notice for repairs, inspections, or showing to future tenants or buyers, except in the case of emergency where notice is not required.

8. OCCUPANCY LIMITS. Only those listed on the rental application may reside at the premises unless Landlord gives written consent for additional occupants. Subletting is not permitted without Landlord’s approval.

9. PETS. No pets are allowed unless Landlord provides prior written approval. If allowed, Tenant agrees to follow all pet care rules and pay any applicable pet fees.

10. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Washington State Department of Health information on mold and will maintain the property to prevent mold growth by ventilating and promptly reporting water leaks.

11. TERMINATION AND MOVE-OUT. Tenant must give 30 days’ written notice before moving out. The premises must be left in clean condition, and all keys returned. Any damage or unpaid rent will be deducted from the security deposit as described above.
