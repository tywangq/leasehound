This Residential Lease Agreement is made and entered into by and between Mike Williams ("Landlord"), whose address is 1002 Pacific Ave, Tacoma, WA 98402, and Jamie Chen ("Tenant"), for the premises located at 1223 S. Juniper St, Tacoma, WA 98405 (the "Premises").

1. TERM AND RENT. The lease term begins on July 1, 2024 and continues month-to-month, with rent of $2200 due on the first day of each month. Rent shall be paid by check or electronic transfer to the Landlord.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant will pay a security deposit of $2200, held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 424 St. Helens Ave, Tacoma, WA 98402. The deposit is not to be used for rent. It may only be withheld to cover unpaid rent or to repair damages beyond normal wear and tear, or as otherwise allowed by law.

3. CONDITION OF PREMISES. At move-in, Tenant and Landlord will sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the Premises, including existing damage or deficiencies. Tenant acknowledges receipt of a copy of this checklist.

4. MOVE-IN INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information about fire safety and how to operate and maintain smoke detectors. Tenant also acknowledges receipt of the Washington Department of Health’s information on mold and ways to control its growth.

5. USE OF PREMISES. The Premises shall be used by no more than two adults and two children, and solely for residential purposes. No business or commercial activity is permitted without Landlord's prior written consent.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and promptly notify Landlord of needed repairs. Landlord will be responsible for reasonable repairs not caused by Tenant’s neglect.

7. UTILITIES. Tenant shall pay for all utilities and services to the Premises except garbage service, which will be paid by Landlord.

8. RIGHT OF ENTRY. Landlord may enter the Premises with at least two (2) days’ written notice to make repairs or inspect. For showings to prospective tenants or purchasers, one (1) day’s written notice will be given. In emergencies, no notice is required.

9. LATE RENT. If rent is not paid within five (5) days after it is due, a late fee of $75 will be charged. No late fee will be assessed if payment is made within this grace period.

10. TERMINATION OF TENANCY. Tenant may terminate this lease at any time by giving at least twenty (20) days’ written notice before the end of a rental period. Landlord will provide notice as required by state law.

11. ALTERATIONS AND IMPROVEMENTS. Tenant shall not make any alterations, additions, or improvements to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Any improvements made become the property of Landlord unless otherwise agreed in writing.

12. RULES AND REGULATIONS. Tenant agrees to follow reasonable rules established by Landlord for the safety and care of the building and its residents. Landlord will provide written copies of any such rules.
