This Residential Lease Agreement is between Michael Nguyen (Landlord) and Emma Rodriguez (Tenant) for the residential property located at 14236 SE 16th Street, Bellevue, Washington. 1. TERM. The lease begins on July 1, 2024, and will continue on a month-to-month basis until either party provides a written thirty (30) day notice of termination. The Tenant shall vacate the premises promptly upon the lease ending.

2. RENT. The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of $2,950 due on the first (1st) day of each month. Rent not received by the fifth (5th) day of the month will be subject to a $50 late fee. Payments shall be made by check or electronic transfer as directed by the Landlord.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,950, to be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 1101 Pacific Avenue Suite 300, Tacoma, WA 98402. The deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other breaches of this agreement.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION. Upon move-in, the Tenant and Landlord shall complete and sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the unit. This checklist will serve as a reference at the end of the tenancy.

5. UTILITIES. The Tenant is responsible for electricity, water, sewer, and trash services. The Landlord will pay for yard maintenance and property taxes. All utility payments must be kept current by the Tenant.

6. OCCUPANTS. Only the Tenant and approved occupants listed in the rental application may reside at the property. No subletting or assignment is permitted without the written consent of the Landlord.

7. PETS. No pets shall be allowed on the premises without the prior written approval of the Landlord. Pet approval may include an additional deposit and specific pet rules.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. The Tenant must keep the premises clean and promptly notify the Landlord of any needed repairs. Damage from neglect or misuse by the Tenant, their guests, or invitees will be the responsibility of the Tenant.

9. MOLD DISCLOSURE. The Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Department of Health information on mold, including prevention and cleanup. Tenant agrees to promptly report any signs of water intrusion or visible mold growth to the Landlord.

10. TENANT WAIVER OF LEGAL RIGHTS. The Tenant agrees to waive any rights or remedies available under the Residential Landlord-Tenant Act (RCW 59.18) or any applicable law for disputes arising under this lease agreement. Any such issues will be handled privately between the parties.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY OF LEASE TERMS. The Tenant shall not disclose to any third party, or discuss with any third party, the rent amount or any terms of this lease agreement without the written consent of the Landlord, except as required by law.
