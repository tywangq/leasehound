This Lease Agreement is between John R. Martin (Landlord) and Elise Ramos (Tenant) for the residential property located at 442 E. Pine Street, Walla Walla, Washington.

1. TERM AND OCCUPANCY. The tenancy begins July 1, 2024, and continues on a month-to-month basis unless otherwise agreed. Only Tenant and named occupants may reside at the premises.

2. RENT. Tenant agrees to pay $1,875.00 per month, due on the first day of each month. Rent payments shall be delivered to Landlord at 455 E. Alder Street, Walla Walla, WA 99362, or as otherwise instructed in writing.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,875.00. The deposit is held in a trust account at Baker Boyer Bank, 7 W Main St, Walla Walla, WA 99362. The deposit may be withheld only for unpaid rent or to cover repair of damages beyond normal wear and tear.

4. DEPOSIT REFUND. Landlord will provide an itemized statement and refund any remaining security deposit within thirty (30) days after Tenant moves out and returns possession of the premises.

5. FIRE SAFETY INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety and smoke detector information at the time of signing this agreement.

6. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Washington State Department of Health informational pamphlet on indoor mold.

7. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for all utilities except water and sewer, which will be paid by Landlord. Tenant agrees to set up accounts in their own name by move-in.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall maintain the premises in a clean and sanitary condition and promptly notify Landlord of any required repairs. Landlord will address repair requests in a timely manner, consistent with state law.

9. DAMAGE BY TENANT. Tenant is responsible for damage to the premises caused by Tenant's own negligence, and for any damage caused by the actions or negligence of their guests, beyond ordinary wear and tear.

10. HOUSE RULES. Landlord may adopt or amend reasonable house rules for the property, provided that Tenant receives written notice before any new or amended rule becomes effective.

11. TERMINATION BY TENANT. Tenant may terminate this agreement on at least twenty (20) days' written notice before the end of a rental period, as required by law.

12. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises for inspection, repairs, or to show the unit to prospective tenants or buyers, only after providing at least two (2) days’ written notice to Tenant, except in emergency situations as allowed by Washington law.
