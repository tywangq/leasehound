This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of June 15, 2024, by and between Yakima Property Management LLC ("Landlord"), and Jamie L. Rivera ("Tenant"), for the premises located at 512 South 12th Avenue, Unit 2, Yakima, WA 98902 ("Premises").

1. TERM AND POSSESSION. The term of this Agreement shall commence on July 1, 2024, and continue on a month-to-month basis unless terminated according to the provisions herein. Tenant shall be entitled to possession of the Premises as of the commencement date, provided all required payments and documentation, including a signed move-in condition checklist, are delivered to Landlord.

2. RENT PAYMENT. Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent in the amount of $1,750.00 due in advance on the first (1st) day of each month, commencing July 1, 2024. All rent payments must be made exclusively via Zelle transfer to Landlord’s designated account; no checks, money orders, cash, or alternative payment methods shall be accepted under any circumstances, and failure to comply shall be deemed a material breach of this Agreement.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant has deposited $1,750.00 as a security deposit, to be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 1200 S. 72nd Avenue, Yakima, WA 98908. The deposit may be withheld or deducted to cover unpaid rent, cleaning costs beyond ordinary wear and tear, repair of damages in excess of normal use, or restoration of the Premises to original condition upon move-out as documented in the move-in checklist.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Upon occupancy, Tenant has reviewed, signed, and received a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the Premises, including existing damages and the status of all appliances, which shall serve as a basis for determining the return of any security deposit at the end of tenancy.

5. UTILITIES AND SERVICES. Tenant shall be solely responsible for timely payment of all utilities and services for the Premises, including but not limited to electricity, water, sewer, gas, and trash removal, except for lawn maintenance, which shall be provided by Landlord at Landlord’s discretion and schedule.

6. FIRE SAFETY INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written notice regarding fire safety features and evacuation routes for the Premises, as well as detailed information concerning the type, location, and proper maintenance of smoke detectors.

7. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant affirms receipt of the Washington State Department of Health informational pamphlet regarding the prevention, identification, and remediation of mold in residential dwellings.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and in good order, promptly notify Landlord in writing of any need for repairs, and shall not make alterations or improvements without the prior written consent of Landlord. Landlord shall be responsible for major structural repairs not caused by Tenant’s misuse, while Tenant shall bear the cost of damage resulting from negligence or deliberate acts.

9. WAIVER OF JURY TRIAL AND CLASS ACTION. Tenant hereby irrevocably waives any and all right to participate in a class action or a jury trial pertaining to any dispute, claim, or cause of action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the tenancy, and agrees that any such matter shall be resolved on an individual basis exclusively between Landlord and Tenant.
