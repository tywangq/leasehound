This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into by and between Maria Alvarez ("Landlord") and Samuel Lee ("Tenant") for the premises located at 529 Maple Avenue, Apartment 3B, Yakima, WA 98902. This Lease commences on July 1, 2024, and continues on a month-to-month basis after the initial term of one year.

1. PREMISES AND OCCUPANCY. The Tenant shall have the right to exclusively occupy the above-referenced apartment for residential purposes only. Occupancy is limited to no more than four persons unless written consent is provided by the Landlord.

2. TERM. The Lease term shall begin on July 1, 2024, and last for twelve (12) months, ending on June 30, 2025. Thereafter, the Lease shall continue on a month-to-month basis unless either party provides thirty (30) days’ written notice to terminate.

3. MONTHLY RENT. Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of $2,950, due on or before the first day of each month. Payments shall be made by check or electronic transfer to the Landlord at the address specified in this Lease or as otherwise directed in writing.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,950 upon execution of this Lease. The deposit is held in a trust account at Yakima Valley Bank, 118 East Yakima Avenue, Yakima, WA 98901. The security deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other lawful reasons described in this Lease.

5. USE OF PREMISES. The Tenant shall keep the premises clean and in good condition, and shall not engage in any unlawful activities or conduct that would disturb neighbors. No alterations or painting may be done without written consent from the Landlord.

6. UTILITIES. The Tenant is responsible for payment of all utilities, including electricity, internet, water, and trash disposal, unless otherwise specified in writing by the Landlord. Landlord shall pay for sewer services.

7. ENTRY AND INSPECTION. Landlord may enter the premises for repairs, inspections, or showings by providing at least 48 hours’ notice, except in emergencies where immediate access is required. Entry shall occur at reasonable hours.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord of any condition requiring repair. Landlord is responsible for repairs except those necessitated by Tenant’s negligence or willful misconduct, which shall be at Tenant’s expense.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY OF LEASE TERMS. Tenant shall not disclose or discuss the rent amount or any of the terms and conditions of this Lease with third parties, except as required by law or with written permission from the Landlord. Any breach of this confidentiality obligation may result in termination of tenancy.    

10. WAIVER OF RIGHTS UNDER LAW. Tenant hereby waives any and all rights or remedies that may be provided under the Washington Residential Landlord-Tenant Act (RCW 59.18) or any applicable law to the extent permissible. This waiver applies fully to the relationship between Landlord and Tenant under this Lease.

11. FIRE AND MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information regarding fire safety, smoke detectors, and the Washington State Department of Health’s document on mold. Tenant is responsible for regularly testing smoke detectors and promptly notifying Landlord of any malfunctions.

12. TERMINATION AND RETURN OF POSSESSION. Upon termination or expiration of the Lease, Tenant shall return keys and vacate the premises in clean condition, with removal of all personal belongings. A forwarding address must be provided for return of the security deposit as detailed in this Lease.
