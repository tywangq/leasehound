This lease agreement is made between John D. Landry (hereafter referred to as "Landlord") and Samantha R. Pierce (hereafter referred to as "Tenant") for the residence located at 1415 Cypress Avenue, Everett, Washington. 

1. TERM OF LEASE. This lease will begin on May 1, 2024, and shall continue on a month-to-month basis unless terminated with proper written notice by either party in accordance with Washington State law. 

2. RENT AMOUNT AND PAYMENT. The monthly rent is $2,200, due on the first day of each month. Rent should be paid by check or electronic transfer to the Landlord's designated account. 

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,200 before move-in. The deposit is held in a trust account at Coastal Community Bank, 10520 19th Ave SE, Everett, WA 98208. The deposit may be withheld to cover unpaid rent or to repair damages caused by Tenant beyond normal wear and tear. Any deductions will be itemized, and the balance (if any) returned as specified by law. 

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION. Upon move-in, both Landlord and Tenant will complete and sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises, which shall be used to evaluate the return of the security deposit at move-out. 

5. LATE FEES. If rent is not received by the Landlord within five days after the due date, a late fee of $75 will be charged. No late fee will be assessed if the full rent is received within the five-day grace period. 

6. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for payment of all utilities, including water, electricity, and garbage collection, unless otherwise agreed in writing. Landlord is not liable for interruptions in service due to utility provider actions or maintenance. 

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant agrees to keep the property clean and to promptly notify Landlord of any needed repairs. Tenant is responsible for any damage caused by their own negligence, excluding ordinary wear and tear. Landlord will handle repairs not caused by Tenant as required by law. 

8. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises only with at least two days' notice to the Tenant, except in cases of emergency, to inspect, make repairs, or show the unit to prospective tenants or buyers as allowed by Washington law. 

9. PET POLICY. No pets are allowed on the property without the prior written approval of the Landlord. If Tenant wishes to keep a pet, a written agreement and an additional deposit will be required. 

10. TERMINATION AND MOVE-OUT. Tenant must provide at least 20 days' written notice before vacating the unit. Upon moving out, Tenant shall return all keys and leave the property in the same condition as documented in the move-in checklist, aside from reasonable use and wear. The security deposit will be returned or deductions itemized and explained within 21 days as required by law.
