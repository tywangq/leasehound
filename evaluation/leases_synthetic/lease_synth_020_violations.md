This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between John L. Harper ("Landlord") and Samantha K. Daniels ("Tenant"), for the lease of the premises located at 2206 E 15th Avenue, Kennewick, Washington 99337 (the "Premises") within the city of Kennewick. This Agreement is effective as of June 1, 2024.

1. TERM. The term of this lease shall commence on June 1, 2024 and shall continue on a month-to-month basis thereafter unless terminated by either party upon thirty (30) days' written notice, subject to all provisions herein.

2. RENT. The monthly rent shall be one thousand four hundred fifty dollars ($1,450.00), due and payable in advance on the first (1st) day of each month at the address designated by Landlord.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall pay a security deposit in the amount of one thousand four hundred fifty dollars ($1,450.00), to be held in a trust account at Columbia Bank, 3250 W Clearwater Ave, Kennewick, WA 99336. Tenant has received and signed a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the Premises at move-in.

4. USE OF PREMISES. The Premises shall be occupied only by Tenant and such other persons as are listed in writing and approved by Landlord. No commercial activity shall be conducted on the Premises.

5. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the Premises, with or without Tenant's presence, for purposes of inspection, repair, or showing the unit to prospective buyers or tenants, provided that at least twenty-four (24) hours' advance notice is given to Tenant, except in cases of emergency.

6. SHORT NOTICE ENTRY. Notwithstanding any other provision, Landlord may enter the Premises with twelve (12) hours' advance notice to Tenant for repairs or to inspect the condition of the Premises. In cases of emergency, no notice shall be required.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and in good order. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord of any damage or necessary repairs. Ordinary wear and tear excepted, damages caused by Tenant or Tenant’s guests shall be repaired at Tenant’s expense.

8. UTILITIES. Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all utilities and services for the Premises except for water, sewer, and garbage, which shall be provided by Landlord.

9. SMOKE DETECTORS AND FIRE SAFETY. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information regarding fire safety, evacuation routes, and smoke detectors, and agrees to test and maintain all smoke detectors as required by law.

10. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Department of Health's information on mold and agrees to follow recommended practices for mold prevention and remediation.

11. EXCULPATION AND HOLD HARMLESS. Tenant agrees that Landlord shall not be liable for any injury to Tenant or Tenant’s guests or for any loss or damage to personal property occurring on the Premises, even if such injury or damage results from the negligence of Landlord. Tenant shall hold Landlord harmless from any claims, demands, or damages arising from Tenant's use or occupancy of the Premises.

12. ATTORNEY FEES AND COSTS. In the event of any legal dispute or litigation arising from this Agreement, Tenant shall be responsible for all attorney fees, court costs, and legal expenses incurred by Landlord, regardless of which party prevails in such action.

13. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not assign this Agreement or sublet the Premises or any part thereof without the prior written consent of Landlord. Any unauthorized assignment or subletting shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.
