This Residential Lease Agreement is made between Daniel Spruce ("Landlord") and Jamie Lin ("Tenant") for the property at 1823 Roeder Avenue, Bellingham, Washington.

1. RENT AND TERM. Tenant agrees to rent the above premises for a term beginning July 1, 2024, and continuing month-to-month thereafter. The rent is $1100 per month, due on the first day of each month at the address specified by Landlord.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1100. The deposit is held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 2930 Newmarket St Suite 101, Bellingham, WA 98226. The deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other defaults under this lease. An itemized statement of any deductions will be provided as required by law.

3. MOVE-IN CONDITION AND CHECKLIST. At move-in, Tenant and Landlord will sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises. This checklist will be used to determine any deductions from the deposit at move-out.

4. PET DAMAGE DEPOSIT. If Tenant keeps a pet on the premises, an additional refundable pet damage deposit of $250 is required. This deposit may be withheld or deducted for pet-related damages beyond normal wear and tear; any remainder will be returned as provided by law.

5. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for payment of all utilities and services for the premises, except water and garbage, which are paid by Landlord.

6. OCCUPANTS AND USE. The premises are to be used only as a private residence by the Tenant and those listed on the rental application. No subletting or additional occupants are allowed without Landlord’s written permission.

7. HOUSE RULES. The Tenant agrees to abide by all reasonable house rules as established and amended from time to time by Landlord. Any amendments to house rules will be provided to Tenant in writing with reasonable advance notice.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant will keep the premises clean and in good condition and promptly notify Landlord of any repairs needed. Tenant will not make alterations without Landlord’s written permission.

9. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises for inspection, repairs, or to show the unit to prospective tenants or buyers, with at least 48 hours’ notice except in case of emergency.

10. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTORS. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information regarding fire safety and smoke detector operation, as required by law.

11. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Department of Health’s information pamphlet regarding mold and proper ventilation of the premises.
