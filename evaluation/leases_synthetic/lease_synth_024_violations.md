This residential lease agreement is made between Sam Cartwright ("Landlord") and Jamie Lin ("Tenant") for the rental of the single-family home located at 15804 NE 18th Street, Bellevue, Washington.

1. RENT. Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent of $2,400, due on the first day of each month. Rent is considered late if received after the 5th, at which point a late fee of $75 will apply. All rent payments should include the property address in the memo.

2. TERM. The tenancy begins July 1, 2024 and will continue on a month-to-month basis until either party provides 30 days’ written notice of termination. 

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant will pay a security deposit of $2,400, held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 501 100th Ave NE, Bellevue, WA 98004. At the end of the tenancy, the deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, cleaning costs, or repair of damages beyond normal wear and tear. A written, itemized statement will be provided within 21 days after move-out.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Upon move-in, Tenant and Landlord have completed and signed a written checklist detailing the condition and cleanliness of the unit. Both parties retain a copy for reference at move-out.

5. USE OF PREMISES. The premises are to be used solely as a private residence by no more than four persons. No business, subleasing, or assignment is allowed without the Landlord’s prior written consent.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for all utility bills associated with the premises, including electricity, water, gas, garbage, and internet. Tenant must not allow utility services to lapse during the tenancy.

7. PET POLICY. No pets are permitted on the property without the Landlord’s written approval. Approval may be withheld or conditioned at Landlord’s discretion.

8. RENT CONFIDENTIALITY. Tenant agrees not to disclose or discuss the rent amount, lease terms, or any details of this agreement with any third party, except as required by law or court order.

9. WAIVER OF RIGHTS. Tenant hereby waives any legal rights or remedies under the Residential Landlord-Tenant Act (RCW 59.18) or any applicable law, including but not limited to the right to withhold rent or repair-and-deduct.

10. RENT PAYMENT METHOD. All monthly rent payments must be made exclusively through Zelle to the Landlord’s designated account. No checks, money orders, or cash payments will be accepted for rent at any time.

11. ATTORNEY FEES. In any dispute arising out of this lease, the Tenant is responsible for paying the Landlord’s reasonable attorney fees and legal costs, regardless of the outcome of the dispute or any decision by a court.

12. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTOR INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information regarding fire safety and proper use and upkeep of smoke detectors for the premises.

13. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges that they have received a copy of the pamphlet prepared by the Washington State Department of Health on the hazards of indoor mold and steps for prevention.
