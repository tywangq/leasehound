This residential lease agreement is made between Samuel K. Reynolds (the "Landlord") and Jamie M. Ortega (the "Tenant") for the rental of the property at 1249 Cedar Avenue, Walla Walla, Washington.

1. LEASE TERM AND OCCUPANCY. The lease will begin June 15, 2024, and will run on a month-to-month basis unless terminated by either party with 30 days' written notice. Only the Tenant and approved occupants may reside at the premises.

2. RENT. Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of $1100, due on the first day of each month. Rent is to be paid by check or electronic transfer to the Landlord. Late payments after the fifth day will result in a $50 late fee.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1100, to be held for damages, unpaid rent, or cleaning expenses. The deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, damages beyond normal wear and tear, and cleaning costs necessary to return the property to its move-in cleanliness as documented by the signed condition checklist.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Upon move-in, Tenant and Landlord will complete and sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises. Tenant acknowledges receipt of a copy of this checklist and agrees that it accurately represents the property's state at move-in.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the property clean and in good order. Tenant must promptly notify Landlord of any repairs needed. Tenant is responsible for damage caused by negligence or misuse.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant agrees to pay for electricity, water, and garbage services directly to the providers. Landlord is responsible for sewer and property tax.

7. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTORS. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information regarding fire safety and smoke detector maintenance. Tenant agrees to test smoke detectors regularly and notify Landlord immediately if devices are not working.

8. PET POLICY. No pets are permitted on the premises without prior written consent from the Landlord. Violation of this clause will result in a $250 penalty and possible termination of tenancy.

9. CLASS ACTION WAIVER. Tenant agrees that any legal claims or disputes with the Landlord must be brought individually and not as a participant in any class action or similar representative proceeding. Tenant also waives the right to trial by jury in any such dispute.
