This Lease Agreement is entered into between Samuel Ortega ("Landlord") and Julia Martinez ("Tenant") for the residential premises located at 714 Willow Avenue, Walla Walla, Washington.

1. TERM. The lease begins on July 1, 2024, and continues until June 30, 2025, unless terminated sooner in accordance with this agreement or applicable law. Either party may choose to renew or not renew with 60 days' written notice prior to the end of the term.

2. RENT. Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent in the amount of $2,675. Rent is due on the first day of each month without demand. If rent is not received by the fifth of the month, a late fee of $50 will be charged for each week until full payment is received.

3. ELECTRONIC PAYMENTS ONLY. All rent payments shall be made exclusively through Zelle to the Landlord’s designated account. No cash, checks, money orders, or other forms of payment will be accepted under any circumstances. Tenant is responsible for ensuring payments are processed on time.  

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon signing, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,675. This deposit will be held in a trust account at Baker Boyer Bank, located at 1101 W. Rose St., Walla Walla, WA 99362. The deposit may be withheld to cover unpaid rent, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other lawful deductions as detailed herein.

5. DEPOSIT WITHHOLDING TERMS. The security deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, damages beyond normal wear and tear, costs arising from cleaning beyond that required by the move-in condition, and for any other reasons allowed under Washington law. An itemized written statement will be provided explaining any withholdings.

6. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. At move-in, Tenant received and signed a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises. This checklist, signed by both parties, forms the basis for comparison at move-out.

7. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for all utilities, including water, electricity, garbage, gas, and internet. Tenant agrees to keep all utility accounts current and in their name during the term of this lease.

8. OCCUPANCY LIMITS. The premises shall be occupied only by Tenant and up to two additional persons listed in this agreement. No other persons may reside in the unit without the Landlord's written approval.

9. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the premises clean and in good order and promptly notify Landlord of any damage or condition requiring repair, except in the case of an emergency, in which Tenant must call appropriate services immediately.

10. ENTRY. Landlord will provide at least 48 hours’ advance notice before entering the premises for inspection, repairs, or showings, except in case of emergency when no advance notice is required.

11. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTORS. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety information and instructions regarding smoke detector testing and maintenance. Tenant agrees to test smoke detectors monthly and replace batteries as needed.

12. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Washington State Department of Health information on mold, including proper ventilation, cleaning, and reporting of mold issues.

13. ATTORNEY FEES. If any dispute or legal action arises out of this lease, Tenant shall be responsible for paying all attorney fees and legal costs incurred by Landlord, regardless of the outcome of such dispute or action.
