This lease agreement is made between Sam Jones (Landlord) and Riley Chen (Tenant) for the rental of 4907 E. Sprague Ave, Apartment #3, Spokane, Washington. The lease begins July 1, 2024. 

1. RENT AND PAYMENTS. Tenant agrees to pay Landlord a monthly rent of $2,100, due on the first day of each month. Payment shall be made by check or electronic transfer to the account specified by the Landlord.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,100 before moving in. The deposit will be held in a trust account at River City Bank, 123 N Howard St, Spokane, WA 99201. The deposit is not last month's rent and must be maintained separately by the Landlord.

3. TERM AND RENEWAL. The tenancy will start on July 1, 2024, and continue month-to-month unless either party gives 30 days' written notice of termination. All conditions herein remain in effect for the entirety of the tenancy.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Upon move-in, Tenant and Landlord will complete and sign a written checklist detailing the rental unit’s condition and cleanliness. This document will be used to compare the unit's state at move-out.

5. OCCUPANTS AND USE. Only the Tenant and those listed on the rental application may live in the premises. Tenant shall not sublease or assign the unit without Landlord’s prior written consent. The premises shall be used only as a private residence.

6. RIGHTS WAIVER. By signing this agreement, Tenant agrees to waive any and all rights or remedies that may be available under the Residential Landlord-Tenant Act (RCW 59.18) or any applicable law.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant will keep the premises clean and in good order, promptly notifying the Landlord of any needed repairs. The Landlord will handle repairs due to normal wear and tear; damage caused by neglect or misuse will be the responsibility of the Tenant.

8. ATTORNEY FEES AND LEGAL COSTS. If any legal dispute arises out of this lease, Tenant agrees to pay Landlord's attorney fees and legal costs, whether or not the Landlord prevails in the dispute.

9. FIRE SAFETY INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety information and materials about smoke detector operation and location within the premises.

10. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Department of Health’s informational pamphlet on mold, including proper ventilation and cleaning practices.

11. ENTRY AND NOTICE. Landlord may enter the premises with at least two days' notice for inspections, repairs, or to show the unit, unless in the case of emergency where immediate entry is required.
