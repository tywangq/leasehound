This Residential Lease Agreement is made between John Simmons (Landlord) and Jamie Rivera (Tenant) for the rental of the home at 1810 Maple Lane, Vancouver, Washington.

1. RENTAL TERM. The agreement begins on June 1, 2024 and continues on a month-to-month basis unless terminated according to Washington law. Either party may terminate the lease with at least 20 days’ written notice before the end of a rental period.

2. RENT. The monthly rent is $3,025, due on the first day of each month at Landlord's address or by another agreed method. Rent received after the fifth day of the month may be subject to a late fee in compliance with Washington law.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,025 upon signing this lease. The deposit will be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 902 Washington St, Vancouver, WA 98660. The deposit will not be used for rent unless agreed in writing.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. At move-in, Tenant will complete and sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises, appliances, and fixtures. Both Tenant and Landlord will retain a signed copy of this checklist for their records.

5. OCCUPANTS AND USE. Only those listed on the rental application may reside in the premises unless otherwise agreed in writing by Landlord. The premises shall only be used as a private residence and not for business or commercial activities.

6. HOUSE RULES. Tenant agrees to follow all reasonable house rules regarding quiet hours, trash disposal, and general upkeep. Landlord may amend the house rules with reasonable written notice to Tenant.

7. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises with at least two (2) days’ written notice to inspect, make necessary repairs, or show the unit to prospective tenants or buyers. For showings, Landlord may enter with at least one (1) day's written notice, as permitted by Washington law.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant must keep the premises clean and in good order, and promptly notify Landlord of any needed repairs. Landlord is responsible for maintaining the property according to state and local laws, including all major systems and appliances.

9. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTORS. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information about fire safety and the operation and maintenance of smoke detectors, as required by law.

10. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Department of Health’s informational pamphlet on mold, including proper ventilation and cleaning tips to reduce mold growth.

11. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for payment of all utilities including water, electricity, garbage, and natural gas unless otherwise specified in writing. Landlord will ensure the utilities are in good working order at move-in.

12. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT. Tenant may not sublet or assign the premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Any sublease or assignment without consent is void and may lead to termination of tenancy as provided by law.
