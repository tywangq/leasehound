This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Lease") is entered into this 1st day of May, 2024, by and between Pacific Sound Properties, LLC ("Landlord"), and Jordan L. Phillips ("Tenant"), concerning the residential premises located at 6141 Cedar Street, Tacoma, Washington (the "Premises").

1. TERM. The term of this Lease shall commence on May 1, 2024, and continue on a month-to-month basis unless terminated by either party with thirty (30) days' written notice in accordance with Washington law. Tenant shall vacate the premises at lease end unless otherwise agreed in writing.

2. RENT. Monthly rent in the amount of $2,900.00 shall be due on or before the first (1st) day of each month, payable in advance. Only electronic payments through the Zelle platform will be accepted; cash, checks, or money orders will not be accepted under any circumstances.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon signing this Lease, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,900.00, to be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 1101 Pacific Avenue, Tacoma, WA 98402. The deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, late fees, or for damages to the Premises beyond ordinary wear and tear, with an itemized statement provided to Tenant as required by law.

4. LATE PAYMENT. If rent is not paid in full by the second day after its due date, a late fee of $75.00 will be charged to the Tenant. The late fee is due immediately and is considered additional rent under this Lease.

5. UTILITIES AND SERVICES. Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all utilities and services supplied to the Premises, including but not limited to water, electricity, gas, trash, internet, and cable. Charges for these services shall be paid promptly to avoid disruption.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises in a clean, sanitary, and safe condition. Tenant shall not commit waste or permit damage to the property, and will promptly notify Landlord of any conditions requiring repair. Ordinary wear and tear is excepted from Tenant’s responsibility.

7. CONDITION OF PREMISES. Upon occupancy, both parties shall sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the Premises. This checklist shall be used to determine the condition of the Premises at move-out. Tenant acknowledges receipt of a copy of the checklist.

8. FIRE SAFETY AND MOLD. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety and smoke detector information. Tenant also acknowledges receipt of the Department of Health informational pamphlet on mold prevention and cleanup.

9. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION. Landlord shall not be liable for any injury to Tenant or Tenant’s guests, or for any damage to property occurring on or about the Premises, even resulting from Landlord’s own negligence. Tenant agrees to hold the Landlord harmless from all such claims, except where prohibited by law.

10. ENTRY AND INSPECTION. Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs, or to show to prospective tenants or buyers with at least two (2) days’ notice, except in cases of emergency where immediate entry is required. Tenant shall not unreasonably withhold consent to Landlord’s entry for lawful purposes.
