This Lease Agreement is entered into on the 15th day of June, 2024, by and between Morgan Jenkins ("Landlord") and Casey Ramirez ("Tenant") for the rental of the residential premises located at 512 Willow Avenue, Yakima, Washington. Both parties agree to the following terms and conditions:

1. TERM OF LEASE. The lease will begin on July 1st, 2024, and shall continue on a month-to-month basis unless terminated by either party with at least 20 days' written notice. If either party wishes to end this agreement, proper notice must be given as specified herein.

2. RENT AMOUNT AND PAYMENT. The monthly rent is $2375, due in full on the first day of each month. Rent shall be paid by check, money order, or electronic transfer to the address or account designated by the Landlord. Payments made after the due date will be subject to a late fee as described below.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon signing this lease, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2375. The deposit may be withheld or deducted upon move-out for unpaid rent, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other lawful charges. An itemized statement will be provided if any portion is retained.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. At move-in, Tenant and Landlord will jointly complete and sign a detailed written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the unit and any provided furnishings or appliances. A copy of this checklist shall be provided to Tenant and maintained by Landlord.

5. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTORS. Landlord has provided Tenant with written fire safety information and specific instructions regarding the operation, maintenance, and testing of smoke detectors as required by law.

6. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of educational materials on mold, including information from the Washington State Department of Health on the prevention and control of mold growth in residential housing.

7. UTILITIES AND SERVICES. Tenant shall be responsible for all utility charges associated with the premises, including but not limited to electricity, water, sewer, and garbage collection, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

8. OCCUPANCY LIMITS. The premises shall be occupied only by the Tenant and the following additional residents: Clara Ramirez (child), and no more than two overnight guests at any one time unless prior written consent is given by Landlord.

9. MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITIES. Tenant must keep the premises clean and sanitary and promptly notify Landlord of any conditions requiring repair. Tenant is responsible for damages caused by Tenant’s own negligence or intentional acts.

10. LATE FEE. If rent is not paid within five days after the due date, a late fee of $50 will be assessed. No late fee will be charged during the initial five-day grace period.

11. PET POLICY AND PET DAMAGE DEPOSIT. Pets are permitted only with Landlord’s prior written approval. An additional pet damage deposit of $500 is required. This deposit may be withheld to cover damage caused by pets, excessive soil or odors, or cleaning necessitated by pets beyond ordinary wear and tear.

12. ALTERATIONS. Tenant shall not make alterations, additions, or improvements to the premises without written consent from the Landlord. Any approved alterations become the property of the Landlord upon termination of the lease unless otherwise agreed.

13. HOUSE RULES. Tenant agrees to comply with all house rules provided by Landlord, which may include policies regarding noise, parking, and common areas. Landlord reserves the right to amend house rules with reasonable written notice to Tenant.

14. RIGHT OF ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises after giving at least two days' written notice for purposes of inspection, repairs, or showing the unit to prospective tenants or buyers, except in cases of emergency as provided by law.

15. SUBLETTING. Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet the premises in whole or in part without the prior written consent of Landlord, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.
