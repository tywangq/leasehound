This lease agreement is made between Sam Jenkins ("Landlord") and Jamie Lee ("Tenant") for the rental of the apartment located at 4815 Maple Avenue, Unit 3, Seattle, WA 98105.

1. RENT AND TERM. The monthly rent for the premises is $1,125, due on the first day of each month. The initial term of this lease shall begin on June 15, 2024, and will convert to month-to-month after twelve months unless renewed in writing.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,125. This deposit is held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 925 E Pike Street, Seattle, WA 98122. The deposit may be withheld only for unpaid rent, damages beyond normal wear and tear, or other conditions as allowed by law. A complete list of any withholdings, if any, will be provided at move-out.

3. MOVE-IN CONDITION. Tenant acknowledges receipt and completion of the signed written move-in condition checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises, as required by Washington law.

4. LATE FEES. If rent is not received by the 5th day after it is due, a late fee of $50 will be assessed. There will be no late fee if payment is made within the five-day grace period.

5. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for electricity, internet, and cable services. Landlord pays for water, sewer, and garbage collection.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord in writing of any needed repairs to the premises. Landlord agrees to make all repairs required by law in a timely manner, and Tenant will not be responsible for normal wear and tear.

7. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises with two (2) days' written notice for repairs, inspections, or other lawful reasons, except in emergencies or to show the unit to prospective tenants or purchasers, for which one day's notice will be given.

8. PETS AND PET DAMAGE DEPOSIT. Tenant may keep up to two cats or dogs with Landlord's prior written approval and by paying a refundable pet damage deposit of $250. The pet deposit may only be withheld or deducted for damage to the unit caused specifically by the pet(s) beyond normal wear and tear, as detailed in an itemized statement.

9. FIRE SAFETY INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety and smoke detector information from Landlord at the time of move-in, including instructions on testing and maintaining smoke and carbon monoxide detectors.

10. MOLD NOTICE. Tenant acknowledges receiving the Department of Health's written informational pamphlet on mold, including tips on prevention and cleaning and the health effects of mold exposure.

11. HOUSE RULES. Landlord may from time to time update house rules as necessary, provided reasonable written notice is given to Tenant, and such rules do not conflict with state or local law.

12. SUBLETTING. Tenant may not sublet or assign the premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

13. ALTERATIONS. No alterations or additions may be made to the premises without Landlord's prior written approval. Any approved modifications must be restored to original condition unless otherwise agreed.

14. TERMINATION AND MOVE-OUT. At move-out, Tenant will return all keys and provide forwarding address in writing. Landlord will conduct a move-out inspection and provide an itemized statement for any deductions from the deposit, in accordance with Washington State law.
