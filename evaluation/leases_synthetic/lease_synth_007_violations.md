This Residential Lease Agreement is made and entered into between Jamie Porter ("Landlord") and Rita Sanchez ("Tenant") for the premises located at 1793 Willow Avenue, Kennewick, Washington. Both parties agree to the following terms and conditions:

1. TERM OF LEASE. The lease term shall begin on May 1, 2024, and end on April 30, 2025, unless extended or terminated by mutual agreement in writing. Tenant agrees to vacate the premises at lease end unless a new agreement is reached.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of $1,275.00, due on the first day of each month. Rent payments will be made by check or electronic transfer to the Landlord's designated account. Late payments will incur a $50.00 fee after the fourth (4th) day of the month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon signing this lease, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,275.00. This deposit secures the faithful performance of all terms of this agreement. The security deposit is refundable at the end of the tenancy, subject to the Tenant's compliance with all lease terms and the return of the premises in proper condition.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION. Tenant acknowledges receipt and review of a signed written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises at move-in, and both parties retain a copy for their records. Tenant agrees to return the premises in substantially the same condition, less normal wear and tear.

5. OCCUPANTS AND GUESTS. Only the Tenant and the following persons may reside at the premises: Mateo Sanchez and Lucía Sanchez. Guests may stay no longer than fourteen (14) consecutive days without written consent from the Landlord.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the premises clean and in good order and promptly notify the Landlord of any condition that requires repair. The Landlord is responsible for repairs not caused by Tenant negligence. Tenant shall not alter or improve the premises without written permission from the Landlord.

7. ENTRY BY LANDLORD. The Landlord reserves the right to enter the premises for inspection, repairs, or to show the unit to prospective tenants or buyers. In most cases, Landlord will provide at least twelve (12) hours' advance notice before entry, except in emergencies.

8. UTILITIES. Tenant shall pay for all utilities and services for the premises, including electricity, water, garbage, and internet. Landlord is responsible for property taxes and insurance on the premises.

9. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTORS. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information regarding fire safety, including the proper use of smoke detectors, and agrees to maintain all smoke detectors in working order during the tenancy.

10. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of information provided by the Washington State Department of Health regarding the potential health risks associated with mold, steps to minimize mold growth, and their rights and duties.

11. USE OF PREMISES. The premises shall be used exclusively as a private residence. No commercial activities are allowed on the premises without written approval from the Landlord. Tenant agrees to comply with all laws and local ordinances.

12. PET POLICY. No pets are allowed on the premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord. If a pet is approved, Tenant agrees to abide by all applicable city ordinances and rules relating to pet ownership.
