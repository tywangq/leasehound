This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into by and between Linda T. Green ("Landlord"), whose address for notice is 5639 Bay Ave, Everett, WA 98203, and Steven C. Marshall ("Tenant"), for the lease of the dwelling unit located at 1005 Lombard Avenue, Apartment 204, Everett, WA 98201 (the "Premises"), in the City of Everett, Washington.

1. TERM AND RENT. The term of this Lease shall begin on July 1, 2024, and extend on a month-to-month basis thereafter. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $950, due in advance on the first (1st) day of each month. Rent shall be paid to Landlord at the address specified above unless notified otherwise in writing. Any rent received more than five (5) days after the due date shall incur a late charge of $50.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $950. The security deposit shall be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 2702 Colby Ave, Everett, WA 98201. The deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, repair of damage beyond normal wear and tear, and cleaning necessary to restore the Premises to the condition described in the move-in condition checklist, excluding reasonable wear. Additional terms regarding the security deposit are governed by RCW 59.18.280.

3. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. At the time of occupancy, Landlord and Tenant have completed and signed a written move-in condition checklist accurately describing the condition and cleanliness of the Premises, fixtures, appliances, and any furnishings, a copy of which Tenant acknowledges receipt.

4. OCCUPANCY AND USE. The Premises shall be used solely as a private residence by no more than two (2) persons. Tenant shall not sublet or assign the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

5. ENTRY BY LANDLORD. Landlord or Landlord’s agents may enter the Premises, with at least twenty-four (24) hours’ advance notice to Tenant, to inspect, make repairs, or show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers. In the event of an emergency or if Landlord reasonably believes abandonment has occurred, Landlord may enter at any time without prior notice. (Violation: short_entry_notice)

6. CONFIDENTIALITY OF LEASE TERMS. Tenant agrees not to disclose or discuss the amount of rent or any terms of this Lease with any third party except as required by law. Any breach of this confidentiality provision shall constitute a material breach of this Lease. (Violation: rent_gag)

7. LIMITATION OF LANDLORD’S LIABILITY. To the maximum extent permitted by law, Landlord shall not be liable for, and Tenant shall hold Landlord harmless from, any injury, loss, or damage to person or property occurring on or about the Premises, even if caused by Landlord’s own negligence, except to the extent resulting from Landlord’s willful misconduct. (Violation: exculpation)

8. LEGAL FEES. In any legal action or proceeding relating to the enforcement of this Lease, Tenant shall pay Landlord’s reasonable attorney fees and costs, regardless of which party prevails. (Violation: attorney_fees)

9. FIRE AND MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant hereby acknowledges receipt of written information regarding fire safety and smoke detector operation, and acknowledges receipt of the Washington State Department of Health’s pamphlet on mold, as required by law.
