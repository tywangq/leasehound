This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into on this 1st day of June, 2024, by and between Sarah Jenkins ("Landlord") and Lucas Ramirez ("Tenant") for the premises located at 8614 West Elm Avenue, Spokane, WA 99208 (the "Premises").

1. TERM AND POSSESSION. The term of this lease shall commence on June 1, 2024 and continue on a month-to-month basis thereafter. Tenant shall be permitted to take possession of the Premises on the commencement date after all required payments and forms have been provided.

2. RENT PAYMENT. Tenant agrees to pay rent in the amount of $1,575 per month, due on the first day of each month without demand. Rent shall be payable to the Landlord at the address provided in writing or at another location designated by the Landlord.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,000. The security deposit shall be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, located at 123 Main Street, Spokane, WA 99201. This deposit is intended to secure the performance of Tenant’s obligations under this Agreement.

4. LATE PAYMENT FEE. If any rent due from the Tenant remains unpaid by the first day after it is due, the Tenant shall incur a late fee of $75, which will be added to the rent balance. Timely payment is of the essence of this Agreement.

5. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Upon move-in, Tenant acknowledges receipt of a signed written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the unit. Both Tenant and Landlord shall retain a copy of this checklist, which is incorporated herein by reference.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises in a clean and sanitary condition and promptly notify Landlord of any damage, malfunction, or repairs that may be required. Tenant is responsible for damages caused by their negligence, except for normal wear and tear.

7. OCCUPANTS AND USE. The Premises shall be occupied only by the Tenant and the following individuals: Emily Ramirez. The Premises shall be used for residential purposes only and no business may be operated without written consent of the Landlord.

8. ENTRY BY LANDLORD. Landlord may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs, or showings by providing at least two days’ notice, except in case of emergency as defined by law.

9. MOLD INFORMATION ACKNOWLEDGEMENT. Tenant hereby acknowledges receipt of the informational pamphlet provided by the Washington State Department of Health regarding mold, including how to prevent and control mold growth in residential dwellings.

10. PETS. No pets shall be kept on the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord. If such consent is given, an additional pet deposit and written agreement shall be required.
