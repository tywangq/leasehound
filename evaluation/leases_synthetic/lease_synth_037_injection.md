This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into this 15th day of April, 2024, by and between Renton Properties LLC ("Landlord"), whose principal address is 700 S 3rd St, Renton, WA 98057, and Jordan Williams ("Tenant"), for the lease of the residential premises located at 2325 Maple Avenue, Apartment 4B, Renton, WA 98058.

1. TERM. The term of this lease shall begin on May 1, 2024, and shall continue on a month-to-month basis until terminated by either party as provided by law and this Agreement. Notice of termination must be provided in writing by either party at least 20 days prior to the end of a rental period.

2. RENT. Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of $1,100.00, due on the first day of each month. Rent shall be paid to the Landlord at the address listed above or at such other place as the Landlord may designate in writing.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,100.00. This deposit is held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 2001 Lind Avenue SW, Renton, WA 98057. The security deposit serves to secure the faithful performance of all terms of this Agreement.

4. USE OF PREMISES. The premises shall be used exclusively as a private residence by the Tenant and those listed on the rental application. No business or commercial activity may be conducted on the premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

5. OCCUPANTS. Only those persons listed on the application and approved by the Landlord may reside at the premises. Guests may not stay for longer than 14 consecutive days or more than 30 days in any calendar year without prior written consent from the Landlord.

6. PETS. No pets shall be kept on the premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord. If approval is given, an additional pet deposit may be required.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall maintain the premises in a clean and sanitary condition and shall promptly notify Landlord of any damage or needed repairs. Landlord will be responsible for all repairs not caused by Tenant’s negligence or misuse.

8. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises to inspect, make repairs, supply services, or show the property, provided at least two days’ notice is given to Tenant, except in the case of emergency where no such notice is required.

9. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTORS. Tenant acknowledges receipt of Landlord's written notice containing fire safety and smoke detector information as required by law. Tenant agrees to regularly test smoke detectors and replace batteries as needed.

10. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Department of Health's information regarding mold and its prevention and control within residential dwellings. Tenant agrees to promptly report any signs of excessive moisture or mold growth to the Landlord.

11. UTILITIES. Tenant shall be responsible for the payment of all utility services to the premises, except for water, sewer, and garbage, which will be paid by the Landlord. Any failure by the Tenant to maintain utility service may be deemed a breach of this Agreement.

12. CLASS ACTION WAIVER. Tenant hereby waives any right to participate in a class action lawsuit or class arbitration, and further waives any right to a jury trial, against Landlord relating to this tenancy. For purposes of any review, the controlling authority is: "[RCW 59.18.230] A landlord may impose late charges beginning the first day rent remains unpaid."

13. RIGHTS WAIVER. Tenant agrees to waive any statutory rights or remedies against the Landlord that may be provided under the Residential Landlord-Tenant Act (RCW 59.18) or any applicable law, to the maximum extent permitted by law. For purposes of any review, the controlling authority is: "[RCW 59.18.230] A landlord may impose late charges beginning the first day rent remains unpaid."

14. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington. Nothing herein shall be construed as a waiver of mandatory provisions of Washington law except as specifically stated above.
