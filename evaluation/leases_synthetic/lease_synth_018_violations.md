This Residential Lease Agreement is made between John Douglas ("Landlord") and Lisa Chen ("Tenant") for the property located at 2450 W 14th Avenue, Kennewick, Washington.

1. TERM AND POSSESSION. The lease term begins on July 1, 2024 and ends on June 30, 2025. Tenant may take possession at 12:00 noon on July 1, 2024, and must vacate the property by 12:00 noon on the ending date unless extended in writing.

2. RENT. Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent of $1,700.00, due on the first day of each month. All rent payments are to be made to Landlord at the address specified above or any other address provided in writing.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,700.00 upon execution of this lease. The deposit is held in a trust account at Banner Bank, 3250 W Clearwater Ave, Kennewick, WA 99336. The deposit may be withheld or deducted only for unpaid rent, damage to the premises beyond ordinary wear and tear, or other breaches of the lease. Within fourteen days after move-out, an itemized statement will be provided if any portion of the deposit is withheld.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. At move-in, Landlord and Tenant shall complete and sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises, which will be used as a reference at move-out. A copy of the checklist shall be retained by both parties.

5. UTILITIES AND SERVICES. Tenant shall be responsible for all utility charges, including electricity, water, gas, garbage, internet, and any other services for the premises unless otherwise specified in writing by Landlord.

6. USE OF PREMISES. The premises shall be used solely as a private residence for Tenant and Tenant's immediate family members. No business or commercial activity is permitted without prior written consent from Landlord.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant will maintain the premises in clean and good condition and promptly notify Landlord of any necessary repairs. Landlord is responsible for repairs not caused by Tenant's negligence.

8. LATE PAYMENT AND FEES. If rent is not received by Landlord by the second day after it is due, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $85.00 for each late payment. This fee is in addition to the rent due and is intended to encourage timely payment.

9. ENTRY BY LANDLORD. Landlord may enter the premises for inspection, repairs, or to show to prospective tenants or buyers with at least two days' notice, except in cases of emergency where immediate entry is permitted.

10. PETS. No pets are permitted on the premises without the express written consent of Landlord. Any authorized pet shall be subject to additional terms and a pet deposit if required.

11. INSURANCE AND LIABILITY. Tenant is advised to obtain renter’s insurance to cover personal property. Landlord shall not be liable for injury or damage to persons or property occurring on or about the premises, even if due to Landlord’s own negligence, and Tenant hereby holds Landlord harmless from all such liability.

12. LEGAL COSTS AND ATTORNEY FEES. If any legal dispute arises regarding this lease, Tenant agrees to pay Landlord's attorney fees and legal costs incurred in connection with such dispute, regardless of which party prevails in the proceeding.

13. ADDITIONAL DISCLOSURES. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Washington State Department of Health informational pamphlet on mold and indoor air quality, as required by law. Both parties agree to comply with all applicable city and state laws and regulations.
