This lease agreement is made between Michael R. Evans ("Landlord") and Sophia M. Torres ("Tenant") for the residential property located at 308 S. Irving Place, Kennewick, Washington.

1. RENT AMOUNT AND PAYMENT. Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent of $3,225, due on the first day of each month, payable to the Landlord at the address provided. If rent is not received within five days after the due date, a late fee of $100 will apply, and the Landlord reserves the right to pursue lawful remedies for unpaid amounts.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,225, to be held in a trust account at Banner Bank, 3250 W Clearwater Ave, Kennewick, WA 99336. The deposit may be withheld, in whole or in part, for unpaid rent, damages beyond normal wear and tear, or unpaid utility charges as permitted by law. Landlord will provide an itemized statement of any deductions within 21 days of lease termination.

3. TERM AND POSSESSION. The lease begins May 1, 2024 and ends April 30, 2025. Tenant will take possession on the start date unless otherwise agreed in writing. Holding over beyond the lease term requires Landlord's advance written consent and may result in daily charges.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. At move-in, Tenant and Landlord shall complete and sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the unit and any existing damage. A copy of the signed checklist will be provided to the Tenant.

5. USE OF PREMISES. The property shall be used solely as a private residence by the Tenant and immediate family. No business, subletting, or assignment is permitted without prior written consent from Landlord. No more than four persons may reside at the premises at any time.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant will keep the unit clean and in good order, and promptly notify the Landlord of any needed repairs. Tenant is responsible for damages caused by their negligence or that of their guests, pets, or invitees, except for ordinary wear and tear.

7. RENT AND LEASE CONFIDENTIALITY. Tenant agrees not to disclose, discuss, or publish the monthly rent amount or any other terms of this lease with any third party, including on social media or to other tenants, except as required by law. Violation of this confidentiality provision may result in termination of tenancy.

8. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTOR INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receiving written fire safety and smoke detector information, including instructions for testing and maintenance, from the Landlord at move-in.

9. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Washington State Department of Health information on mold, including prevention and cleaning guidance, at the time of move-in. Tenant agrees to promptly report any water leaks or visible mold to the Landlord.

10. WAIVER OF CLASS ACTION AND JURY TRIAL. By signing this lease, Tenant agrees to waive participation in any class action lawsuit and any right to a jury trial against the Landlord regarding disputes arising from this lease or tenancy. All disputes must be pursued on an individual basis only and resolved as provided by applicable Washington law.
