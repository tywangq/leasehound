This Residential Lease Agreement is entered into on the 10th day of March, 2024, by and between Madison Grant ("Landlord") and Taylor Chen ("Tenant") for the lease of the property located at 1728 Pine Street, Unit 3B, Seattle, WA 98122 (the "Premises").

1. TERM AND POSSESSION. The tenancy will begin on April 1, 2024, and continue on a month-to-month basis. Tenant shall take possession on the start date, and any extension or renewal shall be in writing and signed by both parties.

2. RENT. The monthly rent shall be $2,125, payable in advance on or before the first day of each month to the Landlord at 5100 Queen Anne Ave N, Seattle, WA 98109, or another place designated in writing. Rent received after the 4th of the month shall incur a $40 late fee.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,125 upon signing this lease. The deposit will be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 401 Union St, Seattle, WA 98101. The deposit may be withheld or deducted only for unpaid rent, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or costs due under this agreement, as detailed in RCW 59.18.280.

4. DEPOSIT RETURN. An itemized statement of damages and any deduction, along with any remaining deposit funds, will be mailed to the Tenant's last known address within 30 days after moving out and returning keys to the Landlord.

5. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Upon move-in, Tenant and Landlord shall complete, sign, and retain a written checklist detailing the condition and level of cleanliness of the Premises. This checklist will be used to assess any potential deductions from the security deposit at move-out.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for payment of electricity, internet, and cable. Landlord will pay water, sewer, and garbage. Tenant agrees to keep all utility accounts in good standing and promptly notify Landlord of any utility issues.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and in good condition and shall promptly notify Landlord in writing of any needed repairs. Landlord shall be responsible for repairs not caused by tenant negligence.

8. TERMINATION OF TENANCY. Tenant may terminate this tenancy by providing at least 20 days’ written notice prior to the end of a monthly rental period. Landlord may terminate per the reasons and notice periods required by RCW 59.18.

9. HOUSE RULES. Tenant agrees to comply with reasonable house rules, which may be amended by Landlord with reasonable written notice to Tenant. House rules will not conflict with Tenant rights under Washington law.

10. FIRE SAFETY AND MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety information, including the location of smoke detectors and emergency exits, and a copy of the Washington State Department of Health's information on mold, as required by law.

11. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the Premises after giving Tenant at least two days’ written notice, except in emergencies or as otherwise permitted by law. Entry will be for inspection, repairs, or to show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers, in accordance with RCW 59.18.
