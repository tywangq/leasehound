This Residential Lease Agreement is made and entered into this 5th day of June, 2024, by and between Evergreen Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Michael Johnson ("Tenant") concerning the residential premises located at 512 Birch Avenue, Yakima, Washington, 98902.

1. TERM. The lease shall commence on June 10, 2024, and continue on a month-to-month basis thereafter unless terminated by either party with a minimum 30 days’ written notice. Early termination by the Tenant without proper notice may result in liability for rent through the notice period.

2. RENT. Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of $1200, due on the first day of each month, at the Landlord’s address or at any place the Landlord designates in writing. Payments received after the fifth day of the month will incur a late fee of $50, and continued late payments may be grounds for termination.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon signing, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1200. The deposit is held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 201 S 2nd Street, Yakima, WA 98901. This deposit secures performance of all terms of this lease. Interest earned, if any, accrues to the Tenant per local law.

4. OCCUPANCY. The premises shall be occupied only by Michael Johnson and his immediate family members as listed on the rental application. Any additional occupants require the written consent of the Landlord and may result in an increase in rent.

5. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Tenant acknowledges that prior to occupancy, both parties completed and signed a written move-in condition checklist that accurately describes the unit’s condition and cleanliness. Tenant retains a copy of the signed checklist for reference.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the premises clean, sanitary, and in good condition, and shall promptly notify Landlord of any required repairs. Damage caused by Tenant’s negligence is the responsibility of the Tenant, and prompt repairs must be made to prevent further damage or possible eviction.

7. UTILITIES. Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all utilities and services to the premises, except for sewer and garbage, which shall be paid by Landlord. Tenant agrees to transfer all necessary utility accounts into their name within three days of occupancy.

8. FIRE AND MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety and smoke detector information as well as the Department of Health informational pamphlet on mold, including instructions for proper ventilation and cleaning techniques to prevent mold accumulation.

9. RULES AND REGULATIONS. Tenant agrees to comply with all house rules provided by the Landlord, including regulations on noise, parking, and waste disposal, and acknowledges that violation of these rules can result in termination of tenancy upon proper notice.

10. ALTERATIONS. No alterations, painting, or significant improvements shall be made to the premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Any fixtures or improvements made without approval may be removed at Tenant’s expense.

11. ATTORNEY FEES. In the event of any dispute, claim, or lawsuit arising out of or relating to this Lease, Tenant agrees to pay all attorney fees and legal costs incurred by the Landlord, regardless of which party prevails in such matter.
