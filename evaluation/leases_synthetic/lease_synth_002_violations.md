This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on this 15th day of June, 2024, by and between Bellevue Property Management LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan Lee ("Tenant") for the premises located at 12916 NE 10th Place, Apartment 410, Bellevue, Washington.

1. TERM OF LEASE. The term of this Lease shall commence on July 1, 2024, and shall continue on a month-to-month basis until terminated by either party with thirty (30) days' written notice as required by Washington law.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay to Landlord monthly rent in the amount of $3,250.00, payable in advance on or before the first (1st) day of each month. Rent shall be paid to Bellevue Property Management LLC at 765 Bellevue Way NE, Suite 200, Bellevue, WA 98004, or at another place designated by Landlord in writing.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,250.00, to be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 2015 156th Avenue NE, Bellevue, WA 98007. The deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, repair of damages beyond normal wear and tear, or other breaches as permitted by law, and a written itemized statement will be provided by Landlord.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Prior to occupancy, Tenant and Landlord shall complete and sign a written checklist or statement describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises, existing damages, and the condition of furnishings and appliances, with a copy provided to each party as required by Washington law.

5. OCCUPANTS. No person other than the Tenant and those listed on the application as residing in the unit shall occupy the premises without prior written consent of Landlord, except for temporary guests not to exceed fourteen (14) consecutive days.

6. ENTRY AND INSPECTION. Landlord or authorized agents may enter the premises to inspect, make necessary repairs, or show the property. Except in case of emergency, Landlord shall provide at least twelve (12) hours' notice prior to entry.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall maintain the premises in clean and sanitary condition and shall promptly notify Landlord of any damages or necessary repairs. Tenant shall be responsible for damages caused by their negligence or that of their guests.

8. UTILITIES. Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all utilities and services supplied to the premises, including but not limited to electricity, water, sewer, and trash, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

9. PETS. No animals or pets shall be kept on or about the premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord, which consent may be withheld at Landlord's sole discretion.

10. ALTERATIONS. Tenant shall not make any alterations, additions, or improvements to the premises without the prior written consent of Landlord. Any alterations made shall become the property of the Landlord at the expiration or termination of this Lease.

11. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTORS. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety and smoke detector information as required by law, and agrees to maintain all smoke detectors in good working order during the tenancy.

12. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Washington State Department of Health information on mold and agrees to practice proper ventilation and cleanliness to minimize the risk of mold growth.

13. TERMINATION AND SURRENDER. Upon termination or expiration of this Lease, Tenant shall vacate the premises, return all keys, and leave the unit in substantially the same condition as at move-in, reasonable wear and tear excepted.
