This residential lease agreement is made between Janet Ellis (Landlord) and Samuel Turner (Tenant), for the rental of the property at 1424 Maple Avenue, Renton, Washington. 

1. TERM AND RENT. The tenancy starts on July 1, 2024, and continues month-to-month. Monthly rent is $1,150 and is due on the first day of each month without demand at the payment location provided by landlord. If the tenant chooses to move out, a written 20-day notice must be given prior to vacating.

2. RENTAL PAYMENTS VIA ZELLE ONLY. Rent must be paid exclusively through Zelle transfers to the bank account specified by the landlord. No other forms of payment, including cash, checks, or money orders, are accepted under this agreement.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,000, to be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 1516 Northwest 56th Street, Seattle, WA 98107. The deposit may be withheld to cover unpaid rent, repair of damages beyond normal wear and tear, or cleaning charges as consistent with state law.

4. LATE FEES FOR RENT. If rent is not received by the landlord on the first day after it is due, a late fee of $40 will be charged for each occurrence. The fee is imposed on the first day rent is late and applies for each month such a delay occurs.

5. TENANT'S WAIVER OF LEGAL REMEDIES. Tenant knowingly agrees to waive any legal rights or remedies provided under the Residential Landlord-Tenant Act (RCW 59.18) or any applicable law in connection with this rental agreement.

6. LANDLORD LIABILITY WAIVER. The landlord will not be liable for injury, damage, or loss occurring on the premises, including those caused by landlord's own negligence. Tenant agrees to hold the landlord harmless from any such instances or claims that arise during the tenancy.

7. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Upon move-in, tenant and landlord will sign a written checklist detailing the condition and cleanliness of the unit. This checklist will be referenced at the end of tenancy when determining if any deductions will be made from the security deposit.

8. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTOR NOTICE. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety information, including operation and upkeep of smoke detection devices, provided by the landlord at lease signing.

9. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receiving the Washington State Department of Health's informational pamphlet regarding mold, including prevention and cleaning tips, as required by law.
