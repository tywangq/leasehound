This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Lease") is entered into this 13th day of March, 2024, by and between Juniper Property Management, LLC ("Landlord"), and Taylor S. Emerson ("Tenant"), for the lease of the residential premises located at 219 Ellis Street, Apartment 104, Bellingham, Washington (the "Premises").

1. TERM AND OCCUPANCY. The term of this Lease shall commence on April 1, 2024, and shall continue for twelve (12) full calendar months, terminating on March 31, 2025, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the terms herein. Occupancy of the Premises is restricted to the Tenant and no more than one (1) additional resident approved in writing by Landlord.

2. RENT. Tenant agrees to pay to Landlord a monthly rent of One Thousand Three Hundred Twenty-Five Dollars ($1,325.00), due in advance on the first (1st) day of each month at Landlord’s address or other place designated in writing. Any partial payment shall be applied to outstanding obligations at Landlord’s discretion.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of One Thousand Two Hundred Dollars ($1,200.00), to be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 1336 Cornwall Avenue, Bellingham, WA 98225. The security deposit may be withheld, in part or in whole, to cover unpaid rent, repair of damages to the Premises beyond normal wear and tear, or for any other lawful purpose as described in this Lease. Within twenty-one (21) days of lease termination and surrender of the Premises, Landlord shall provide Tenant with an itemized list of any deductions and the balance of the deposit, if any.

4. MOVE-IN CONDITION. Upon or before taking possession of the Premises, Tenant and Landlord shall complete a written move-in checklist, to be signed by both parties, which documents the condition and cleanliness of the Premises at commencement of the tenancy. This statement shall be used to assess the state of the Premises upon move-out.

5. PAYMENT OF UTILITIES. Tenant shall be responsible for all utilities except water, sewer, and garbage, which shall be paid by Landlord unless otherwise agreed in writing. Tenant shall place utility accounts in Tenant’s name where applicable.

6. USE OF PREMISES. The Premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes and no commercial activity is permitted. Tenant shall comply with all governmental laws, ordinances, and regulations during the tenancy.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR. Tenant shall maintain the Premises in a clean and sanitary condition at all times. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord of any damage, defect, or needed repairs, and shall be responsible for the cost to repair any damage caused by Tenant’s negligence or that of Tenant’s guests.

8. ALTERATIONS. Tenant shall not make any alterations, additions, or improvements to the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord. Any permitted alterations shall become the property of Landlord upon termination of this Lease unless otherwise agreed.

9. ACCESS BY LANDLORD. Landlord or Landlord’s agents may enter the Premises for inspection, repairs, or showings, with at least two (2) days’ advance notice, except in the case of emergency where no notice is required. Entry shall occur at reasonable times.

10. PETS. No pets or animals shall be kept on the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord. Any approved pet shall be subject to additional terms and conditions at Landlord’s sole discretion.

11. WAIVER OF CERTAIN RIGHTS. Tenant hereby voluntarily waives any and all rights, remedies, or defenses available under the Residential Landlord-Tenant Act of the State of Washington (RCW 59.18) or any applicable law to the fullest extent permitted. This waiver applies to all matters regarding the tenancy and enforcement of this Lease.

12. LATE PAYMENT FEE. If rent is not received by Landlord on the first day after it is due, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $75.00. The late fee shall be imposed beginning on the first day that rent is late, and an additional fee of $10.00 shall accrue for each successive day until the rent is paid in full.

13. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES. In the event of default by Tenant, including but not limited to nonpayment of rent or violation of any Lease provision, Landlord may pursue all remedies available at law or in equity, including termination of tenancy and recovery of damages. Acceptance of partial rent shall not constitute a waiver of Landlord’s rights.
