This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of July 1, 2024, by and between Olympia Residential Holdings, LLC ("Landlord"), whose address is 1208 Union Ave SE, Olympia, WA 98501, and Jane Renter ("Tenant"), whose address upon occupancy shall be 2314 Northpark Drive, Olympia, WA 98501 (the "Premises").

1. TERM. The term of this Agreement shall commence on July 1, 2024, and shall continue on a month-to-month basis unless terminated in accordance with the provisions herein or applicable Washington law. Should Tenant wish to terminate tenancy, Tenant shall provide at least thirty (30) days’ advance written notice to Landlord prior to the effective termination date.

2. RENT. The total monthly rent for the Premises shall be One Thousand Five Hundred Fifty and No/100 Dollars ($1,550.00), payable in advance and due on or before the first day of each calendar month. Rent received after the fifth (5th) day of the month shall be subject to a late charge of fifty dollars ($50.00) plus five percent (5%) of the outstanding balance. There is no grace period beyond the fifth (5th) day.

3. ELECTRONIC PAYMENT OF RENT. All rental payments under this Agreement shall be made exclusively via Zelle electronic transfer to Landlord’s designated account. No other methods of payment, whether by check, money order, cash, or alternative electronic services, will be accepted by Landlord under any circumstances. Tenant bears all responsibility for ensuring timely processing of electronic payments via Zelle.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of One Thousand Two Hundred and No/100 Dollars ($1,200.00) as security for Tenant’s obligations herein. The deposit is held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 412 Lilly Rd NE, Olympia, WA 98506. Upon termination of the tenancy, the deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, damage to the Premises beyond normal wear and tear, costs of cleaning to restore the Premises to its original condition (excluding ordinary cleanliness), and other breaches of this Agreement. Within twenty-one (21) days after Tenant vacates, Landlord shall provide an itemized statement of any amounts withheld and return any balance to Tenant.

5. OCCUPANTS AND USE. The Premises shall be occupied solely by Tenant and those listed on the rental application. The Premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes and shall not be used for any unlawful purpose or in violation of any applicable law, ordinance, or regulation.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant is responsible for maintaining the Premises in a clean, sanitary, and safe condition at all times. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord in writing of any damage, malfunction, or required repair. Tenant shall not make alterations, additions, or repairs without prior written consent from Landlord.

7. UTILITIES. Tenant shall be responsible for payment of all utility charges associated with the Premises, including but not limited to electricity, water, sewer, garbage, internet, and all other services, except that Landlord shall be responsible for property taxes and homeowner's insurance.

8. ENTRY. Landlord or their agents may enter the Premises for inspection, maintenance, or repairs, or to show the Premises to prospective tenants, purchasers, or contractors. Entry shall be upon not less than two (2) days’ advance written notice, except in the event of emergency or abandonment.

9. RULES AND REGULATIONS. Tenant and all occupants and guests shall comply with all rules and regulations promulgated by Landlord, as may be amended from time to time, provided such rules are reasonable and not inconsistent with this Agreement or Washington law.

10. ATTORNEY FEES AND COSTS. In the event any dispute or legal proceeding arises out of or related to this Agreement or the tenancy, Tenant shall be responsible for paying all attorneys’ fees and costs incurred by Landlord, whether or not Landlord prevails in such action or proceeding.

11. INSURANCE. Tenant is encouraged, but not required, to maintain renter’s insurance covering loss of Tenant’s personal property and personal liability. Landlord’s insurance does not cover Tenant’s possessions.

12. FIRE SAFETY INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information from Landlord concerning fire safety and smoke detection devices, in compliance with applicable laws. Tenant agrees to test and maintain smoke detectors as required by law and to notify Landlord promptly in writing of any malfunction or need for replacement.

13. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the current Washington State Department of Health information regarding indoor mold, including prevention and control guidelines. Tenant agrees to take reasonable measures to prevent mold growth, including adequate ventilation and moisture control.

14. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Washington. Any provision herein found to be invalid shall not affect the validity of the remaining provisions.
