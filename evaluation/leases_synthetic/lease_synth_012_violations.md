This Residential Lease Agreement is made between Jane Smith ("Landlord") and Alex Johnson ("Tenant") for the property at 1456 Maple Street, Bellevue, WA.

1. TERM. The lease begins July 1, 2024 and runs on a month-to-month basis unless terminated by either party with at least 20 days' written notice before the end of a rental period.

2. RENT. Tenant will pay $1,925 per month, due on the first day of each month, by check or electronic payment to Landlord at the address provided.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,000 before move-in. Deductions may be made and deposit withheld for unpaid rent, cleaning costs beyond ordinary wear and tear, damage to the premises, or as otherwise permitted by law.

4. LATE FEES. If rent is not received by the landlord on the first day it is due, a late fee of $60 will be charged for each month it is late. Additional rent received after the first day may also incur this late fee.

5. MOVE-IN CONDITION. At move-in, Tenant and Landlord will sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the rental unit. Tenant acknowledges receipt of this checklist.

6. POSSESSION. Tenant will take possession of the premises on July 1, 2024. If Landlord cannot provide possession by that date due to unforeseen circumstances, rent will be prorated accordingly.

7. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises for inspection, repairs, or to show the unit, but will provide at least 24 hours' notice to Tenant in advance, except in case of emergency.

8. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for all utilities except water and garbage, which will be paid for by Landlord.

9. FIRE SAFETY INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information regarding fire safety, including smoke detector maintenance and operation.

10. MAINTENANCE. Tenant shall keep the premises clean and in good condition, and must promptly notify Landlord of any issues needing repair.

11. PETS. No pets are allowed on the premises without written permission from the Landlord.

12. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING. Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet the premises without the Landlord's prior written consent.

13. RULES AND REGULATIONS. Tenant agrees to abide by all house rules and city ordinances affecting the property, including noise limitations and proper trash disposal.

14. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receiving the Department of Health's written information regarding mold and proper ventilation.

15. TERMINATION. Upon move-out, Tenant will return all keys and leave the property in the same condition as at move-in, subject to normal wear and tear. Any damages or cleaning costs may be deducted from the security deposit as provided by law.
