This lease agreement is made between Steve Johnson ("Landlord") and Maria Lopez ("Tenant") for the rental of the residential property located at 4512 NE 73rd Avenue, Vancouver, Washington. This property shall be used only as a private dwelling.

1. RENT AND TERM. The monthly rent for the premises is $1575, due on the first day of each month. The initial term of this lease is twelve (12) months, beginning July 1, 2024 and ending June 30, 2025. If the tenant wishes to renew, a new agreement will be negotiated at least thirty (30) days prior to ending.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1200 upon signing. The deposit is held in a trust account at Riverview Community Bank, 3205 NE 78th Street, Vancouver, WA 98665. The deposit may be withheld or deducted if there is unpaid rent, or for damages to the premises beyond ordinary wear and tear. The deposit will be refunded with an itemized statement of deductions, if any, within thirty (30) days after the tenant moves out.

3. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR. Tenant is responsible for keeping the premises clean and for any repair needed due to the tenant’s or their guests’ negligence. Landlord is responsible for all necessary repairs not caused by tenant’s actions or neglect, including repairs to plumbing, heating, and electrical systems.

4. LATE PAYMENT. If rent is not paid within five (5) days after it is due, a late fee of $50 will be charged. No late fee will be assessed if rent is received within the grace period. Landlord may issue written notice if payment is late and late fees are applied per this clause.

5. ENTRY AND ACCESS. Landlord may enter the premises to make repairs, inspect, or show the property with at least two (2) days’ written notice, except in the case of emergency. Entry will be during reasonable hours and for legitimate purposes only.

6. HOUSE RULES. Tenant agrees to follow all house rules provided by the landlord, such as proper disposal of garbage and quiet hours after 10 p.m. Landlord may amend these rules with at least thirty (30) days’ written notice to the tenant when reasonable.

7. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTOR INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information on fire safety, including evacuation routes, and smoke detector operation as required by law.

8. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Department of Health’s informational pamphlet about mold, including how to prevent and control mold growth inside the home.

9. SUBLETTING AND OCCUPANCY. No subleasing or assignment is allowed without the landlord’s written consent. The premises may not be occupied by more than four (4) persons without written permission from the landlord.
