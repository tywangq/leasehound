This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Lease") is entered into on June 18, 2024, by and between Renton Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Dana C. Morales ("Tenant"). The subject premises are the residential property located at 1418 Maple Avenue NE, Renton, WA 98056.

1. TERM. The lease term shall commence on July 1, 2024, and continue for twelve (12) months, ending June 30, 2025, unless terminated or renewed in accordance with the provisions of this Lease and applicable law. Possession is to be delivered to the Tenant on the commencement date.

2. RENT AMOUNT AND PAYMENT. Monthly rent is $2,425.00, payable in advance on or before the first day of each month directly to the Landlord or as directed in writing. If rent is not received within five (5) days of the due date, it shall be considered late, and such late rent shall be subject to the late fee provisions herein.

3. LATE PAYMENT FEE. If any rent is not paid in full by the first day after it is due, Tenant agrees to pay a late charge of $75.00 for the first day of delinquency and $10.00 for each additional day thereafter, until rent is paid in full. 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,200.00, due at lease signing. The deposit will be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 201 Wells Ave S, Renton, WA 98057. This deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent, damages to the premises beyond normal wear and tear, cleaning beyond that which is required for normal occupancy, or failure to return all keys at move-out as allowed by law.

5. USE OF PREMISES. Premises shall be used solely as a private residence for the Tenant and household members listed herein. No business, commercial, or unlawful uses are permitted on the property or in common areas. Guests may visit for no more than 14 consecutive days without written permission from the Landlord.

6. MOVE-IN CONDITION AND CHECKLIST. Tenant acknowledges receipt of a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the premises at move-in, which has been reviewed and signed by both parties. The checklist shall be used to assess the condition of the property at move-out for the purpose of determining any deductions from the security deposit.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY OF LEASE TERMS. Tenant agrees not to disclose, discuss, or otherwise disseminate the rent amount or lease terms to any third party, except as may be required by law. 8. UTILITIES. Tenant shall be responsible for payment of electricity, natural gas, water, sewer, garbage, cable, and Internet services, and shall maintain accounts in good standing for the duration of the lease. Landlord shall pay for monthly yard maintenance and irrigation services only.

9. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR. Tenant shall keep the premises clean and in good order, and shall promptly notify Landlord of any needed repairs. Landlord will be responsible for repairs not caused by Tenant misuse or neglect. Tenant may be charged for repairs resulting from negligence or intentional damage.

10. PET POLICY. No pets shall be permitted on the premises without prior written approval from the Landlord. A separate pet agreement and deposit may be required if pets are approved. Unauthorized pets are grounds for immediate lease termination.

11. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTORS. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information concerning fire safety and the operation and maintenance of smoke detectors as required by law. Tenant shall not disable or remove any installed smoke or carbon monoxide alarms at any time.

12. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Department of Health informational pamphlet on how to prevent and control mold in residential dwellings. Tenant agrees to promptly report any signs of water intrusion, visible mold, or persistent musty odors to Landlord.

13. RIGHT OF ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises with no less than two (2) days' notice to Tenant for inspection, repairs, or to show the property to prospective tenants or purchasers, except in cases of emergency, when immediate entry is permitted without notice.
