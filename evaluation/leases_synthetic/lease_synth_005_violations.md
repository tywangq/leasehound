This Residential Lease Agreement is made and entered into on June 15, 2024, by and between Hannah Jacobs ("Landlord"), whose mailing address is 123 Evergreen Ave SE, Olympia, WA 98501, and Marcus Lee ("Tenant"), who will reside at 456 Birch St NE, Olympia, WA 98506 (the "Premises") within the city of Olympia, Washington.

1. TERM AND RENTAL AMOUNT. The term of this lease shall begin on July 1, 2024 and continue on a month-to-month basis unless terminated according to this Agreement or as provided by law. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,775, due on the first day of each month, payable to the Landlord at the address listed above or as otherwise specified in writing by the Landlord.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,250 upon signing this lease, to be held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 222 Market St, Olympia, WA 98501. This deposit may be withheld or deducted for unpaid rent or for repairs beyond normal wear and tear, as permitted by law. Landlord will provide the Tenant with a full itemized statement and return any remaining deposit within 21 days of move-out.

3. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. Upon moving in, Tenant will complete and sign a written checklist describing the condition and cleanliness of the Premises. Both Landlord and Tenant will retain copies of this signed checklist, which will be used to assess any deductions from the security deposit at move-out.

4. UTILITIES. Tenant shall be responsible for all utility charges for the Premises, except for garbage collection, which shall be paid by the Landlord. Tenant agrees to maintain timely payment of all utility bills and provide proof of payment upon request.

5. FIRE SAFETY DISCLOSURE. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written information from Landlord regarding fire safety and the operation, testing, and maintenance of smoke detectors in the Premises.

6. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receiving the Washington State Department of Health's informational pamphlet on mold, including prevention and cleaning tips for residential housing.

7. USE AND OCCUPANCY. The Premises shall be used exclusively as a private residence by Tenant and occupants listed in this lease only. No commercial activity or subletting is permitted without prior written consent of the Landlord.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises in a clean and sanitary condition and promptly notify Landlord of any necessary repairs. Tenant shall be responsible for damage caused by negligence or misuse by Tenant or guests, excluding ordinary wear and tear.

9. ENTRY. Landlord reserves the right to enter the Premises after giving Tenant at least two days' notice for inspections, repairs, or showings, except in cases of emergency where immediate access is required for safety or maintenance.

10. WAIVER OF CLASS ACTION AND JURY TRIAL. Tenant agrees that any dispute arising from this Lease or related to the tenancy shall be resolved individually and not as a participant in any class action or representative proceeding. Both Landlord and Tenant further waive the right to a jury trial in any such dispute to the fullest extent permitted by law.
