This Residential Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into this 1st day of July, 2024, by and between Walla Home Property Management LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan M. Rivers ("Tenant") for the premises located at 2037 Alder Street, Walla Walla, Washington 99362 (the "Premises").

1. TERM AND RENT. The term of this Lease shall commence on July 1, 2024, and continue on a month-to-month basis thereafter. The monthly rent shall be $1,775.00, payable in advance on the first day of each month to the Landlord at the address specified herein or as otherwise directed in writing.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,000.00 upon execution of this Agreement. The deposit is held in a trust account at First Sound Bank, 3417 Evanston Ave N, Seattle, WA 98103. Amounts may be withheld from the deposit for unpaid rent, damages beyond normal wear and tear, cleaning costs necessary to restore the premises to the condition at move-in, or other lawful charges as specified in this Lease or by law. 

3. MOVE-IN CONDITION CHECKLIST. At move-in, Tenant and Landlord shall complete a written move-in checklist documenting the cleanliness and condition of the Premises, which shall be signed by both parties. This checklist will be used at move-out to determine any deductions from the security deposit.

4. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and in good order, and promptly notify Landlord in writing of any repair needs. Tenant shall be responsible for damages caused by Tenant’s negligence or misuse. Landlord will be responsible for repairs due to normal wear and tear and major system failures unrelated to Tenant’s actions.

5. OCCUPANTS AND USE. The Premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes and occupied only by those listed on the rental application unless Landlord provides prior written consent. No commercial activity may be undertaken without Landlord’s explicit written permission.

6. PET POLICY. No pets shall be permitted on the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord, which may be withheld or conditioned at Landlord’s sole discretion. Unauthorized pets may result in additional charges and possible termination of tenancy.

7. FIRE SAFETY AND SMOKE DETECTOR INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of written fire safety materials, including escape procedures, and information on smoke detector installation, maintenance, and testing. Tenant agrees to test smoke detectors monthly and promptly report non-functioning units to Landlord.

8. MOLD INFORMATION. Tenant acknowledges receipt of the Washington State Department of Health’s informational pamphlet on mold, including instructions for identification, prevention, and remediation of indoor mold growth.

9. ENTRY AND INSPECTION. Landlord may enter the Premises upon giving at least two days’ notice to Tenant, except in cases of emergency. Entry may be for inspection, repairs, improvements, or to show the Premises to prospective tenants or purchasers.

10. WAIVER OF CLASS ACTIONS. Tenant expressly waives any right to participate in a class action lawsuit or class arbitration, as well as the right to a jury trial, against the Landlord or its agents, in any dispute arising under or relating to this Lease. This waiver does not limit the Tenant’s right to seek relief or damages on an individual basis, as permitted by law.
