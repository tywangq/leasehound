# RCW 59.18.420 — RCW 59.12.090, 59.12.100, 59.12.121, and 59.12.170 inapplicable

Source: https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.420
Jurisdiction: Washington State (Residential Landlord-Tenant Act)

RCW 59.12.090, 59.12.100, 59.12.121, and 59.12.170 inapplicable.

The provisions of RCW 59.12.090, 59.12.100, 59.12.121, and 59.12.170 shall not apply to any rental agreement included under the provisions of chapter 59.18 RCW.

[ 1973 1st ex.s. c 207 s 44.]
