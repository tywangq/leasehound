# RCW 59.18.450 — Relocation assistance for low-income tenants—Payments not considered income—Eligibility for other assistance not affected

Source: https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.450
Jurisdiction: Washington State (Residential Landlord-Tenant Act)

Relocation assistance for low-income tenants—Payments not considered income—Eligibility for other assistance not affected.

Relocation assistance payments received by tenants under *RCW 59.18.440 shall not be considered as income or otherwise affect the eligibility for or amount of assistance paid under any government benefit program.

[ 1990 1st ex.s. c 17 s 50.]
NOTES:

*Reviser's note: The reference in 1990 1st ex.s. c 17 s 50 to "section 50 of this act" is apparently erroneous and has been translated to RCW 59.18.440, which was 1990 1st ex.s. c 17 s 49.
