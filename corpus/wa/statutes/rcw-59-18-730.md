# RCW 59.18.730 — Online landlord resource center

Source: https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=59.18.730
Jurisdiction: Washington State (Residential Landlord-Tenant Act)

Online landlord resource center.

The department of commerce shall create an online landlord resource center to distribute information to landlords about available programs, associated services, and resources including, but not limited to, the following:
(1) The landlord mitigation program created in RCW 43.31.605;
(2) The low-income residential weatherization programs created in chapter 70A.35 RCW; and
(3) Any other programs and resources that the department of commerce determines are relevant.
